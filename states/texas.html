Statewide Organizations12

  1. Texas Civil Rights Projecttxcivilrights.org

    Nonprofit legal organization in Austin, Texas, that does civil rights litigation and advocacy on immigration and border issues, criminal justice, and voting rights. Offices also in Dallas, El Paso, Houston, and the Rio Grande Valley.

  2. La Union del Pueblo Entero (LUPE)lupenet.org

    Membership organization based in San Juan, Texas, that organizes farmworkers, immigrants, and colonia residents in the Rio Grande Valley.

  3. Texas Abolitionistsinstagram.com

    Texas group working to end the death penalty and state killing, support prisoners' rights, and abolish prisons and detention. Offers trainings on abolitionist organizing and prisoner support.

  4. Texas Harm Reduction Allianceharmreductiontx.org

    Austin nonprofit that distributes naloxone and overdose prevention supplies, runs a drop-in center and street outreach, and offers wound care and housing navigation.

  5. Transgender Education Network of Texas (TENT)instagram.com

    Transgender Education Network of Texas (TENT), a statewide trans-led group doing education, policy, and advocacy work for trans people across Texas.

  6. Esto'k Gnainstagram.com

    Esto'k Gna, the Carrizo/Comecrudo Tribe of Texas, works on language revival, cultural practice, and protecting ancestral lands and sacred sites in the Rio Grande Valley.

  7. Texas for Sudaninstagram.com

    Texas for Sudan, dedicated to amplifying Sudanese voices and participation in events centered around Sudanese liberation efforts in Texas.

  8. Texas Equal Access Fundteafund.org

    We're here to help Texans access abortion care by helping them pay for it.

  9. Jane's Due Processjanesdueprocess.org

    Provides free legal help (judicial bypass), a 24/7 hotline, and travel funding so pregnant and parenting minors in Texas can get abortion care and birth control.

  10. Woori Juntoswoorijuntos.org

    Houston-based immigrant justice group serving Asian and Latine immigrants in Harris County through citizenship help, social services, organizing, and advocacy.

  11. Faith in TXfaithintx.org

    Faith in TX organizes and builds power with "the people" so that we "the people" will have greater access to the power to govern our shared society.

  12. 🌹 TSIC 🌹instagram.com
