Statewide Organizations12

  1. Tongva Taraxat Paxaavxa Conservancytongva.land

    The Conservancy's mission includes receiving and managing land back, building community for Native people, practicing traditional ceremonies, creating a native archive, and rematriating the lands by returning our native culture and ecosystems.

  2. Líderes Campesinasliderescampesinas.org

    A grassroots organization led by and for farmworker women, providing mutual aid, leadership development, and organizing support while advocating for workers' rights, gender justice, and environmental health in agricultural communities across California.

  3. Communities for a Better Environment (CBE)cbecal.org

    Environmental justice group organizing in East Oakland, Richmond, Wilmington and Southeast LA, with legal and research work on pollution in low-income communities of color.

  4. Transformative Programming Worksthetpw.org

    Building mutual aid networks and support systems for formerly incarcerated people through community organizing, education, and solidarity initiatives.

  5. Asian Prisoner Support Committee - ROOTS Programasianprisonersupport.com

    Supporting incarcerated Asian, Pacific Islander, and ally communities through ethnic studies education, book programs, correspondence initiatives, and reentry planning across California prisons.

  6. Justice Teams Networkjusticeteams.org

    A California network of local rapid response groups that respond to police and state violence, support affected families, and run healing justice programs, including the California Healers Network.

  7. Power Blossomspowerblossoms.org

    Abolitionist group of queer and trans people inside and outside prison. Runs a letter-writing program, sends care packages, publishes a newsletter, and helps LGBTQ+ people in California with reentry.

  8. California Coalition for Women Prisonerswomenprisoners.org

    We monitor and challenge the abusive conditions inside California women's prisons. We fight for the release of women and trans prisoners. We support women and trans people in their process of re-entering the community.

  9. Dare to Struggleinstagram.com

    Multinational organization with California chapters (SoCal, Bay Area) that organizes against police violence, ICE raids, evictions and homelessness, going door to door and holding speak-outs.

  10. California IWOCinstagram.com

    A union for the incarcerated, supporting their efforts to organize to abolish prisons.

  11. California Immigrant Policy Centercaimmigrant.org

    The California Immigrant Policy Center (CIPC) is a constituent-based statewide immigrant rights organization with offices in Los Angeles, Sacramento and Oakland. It is a leading immigrant rights institution in the state.

  12. Tenants Togethertenantstogether.org

    Statewide California tenant rights organization, founded 2008. Supports tenant unions, provides tenant education resources, and advocates for state housing policy.
