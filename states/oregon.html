Statewide Initiatives

  1. Really Really Free Market Albany ↗ instagram.com

    Free market in Albany that holds events at Hackleman Park where people drop off and take clothes, food and other necessities with no money or trading.

  2. Stop the Sweeps CV ↗ instagram.com

    Small mutual aid group in Corvallis that supports unhoused neighbors with supplies, meals and help during camp sweeps, and advocates against sweeps.

  3. Really Really Free Market Corvallis ↗ instagram.com

    Corvallis free market where people give and take clothes, hygiene items, food, books and other goods at no cost; held on Fridays at Central Park, according to 2021 coverage.

  4. Western WashCo for Racial Justice (WWRJ) ↗ sites.google.com

    An antiracist group in Forest Grove and western Washington County, Oregon, formerly SURJ Forest Grove and now part of the Rural Organizing Project; it holds action meetings, vigils, and local events.

  5. Rogue Action Center ↗ rogueactioncenter.org

    Community organization building grassroots power through mutual aid networks, tenant organizing, and solidarity initiatives across Southern Oregon.

  6. NativeWomanShare ↗ nativewomanshare.com

    Takelma- and Indigenous-led land trust and community project in Murphy, Oregon, running a food sovereignty farm, a gathering space, and supply and produce deliveries for Native women and Two-Spirit people.

  7. Unite Oregon ↗ uniteoregon.org

    Led by people of color, immigrants and refugees, rural communities, and people experiencing poverty, building a unified intercultural movement for justice through community organizing, mutual aid, and leadership development.

  8. Cascades Abortion Support Collective ↗ cascadesabortionsupport.org

    Volunteer-run collective in Portland offering free rides, lodging, meals, emotional support, and aftercare kits to people getting abortions in Oregon, plus free emergency contraception.

  9. Center for Rural Livelihoods ↗ rurallivelihoods.org

    A non-profit organization focused on rural development and community supported forestry, offering workforce training, community garden spaces, and educational programs to support sustainable rural communities.

  10. Clackamas County Equity Coalition ↗ sites.google.com

    A Clackamas County, Oregon coalition that shares newsletters and event calendars, rates candidates on equity, and works on mutual aid, immigrant support, and school board issues.

  11. Respond to Racism ↗ respondtoracism.org

    Lake Oswego nonprofit working on racism in Clackamas County. Holds monthly community meetings and events, offers learning resources, and campaigns for mandatory ethnic studies in high schools.

  12. Rural Organizing Project ↗ rop.org

    A statewide network, founded in 1992, of 85+ local human dignity groups in rural Oregon; it publishes voter guides and hosts an annual Rural Caucus.

  13. Black Oregon Land Trust ↗ blackoregonlandtrust.org

    Black-women-founded community land trust based in Corbett, Oregon. Works to give Black and Indigenous communities secure, affordable land access, with stewardship education and a nature school.

  14. Civil Liberties Defense Center ↗ cldc.org

    Eugene-based nonprofit law office that gives free legal representation to activists, especially climate and protest defendants, and runs legal trainings.

  15. Oregon Sex Workers Committee ↗ oregonswc.org

    Addresses economic and health disparities among consensual adult sex workers and trafficking survivors in Oregon, centering BIPOC communities through research, advocacy, education, and harm reduction to promote safety and equity.

  16. Oregon Rural ↗ oregonrural.org

    People organizing for the well-being of our communities and environment.
