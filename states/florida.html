Statewide Organizations8

  1. Florida Immigrant Coalition (FLIC)floridaimmigrant.org

    Statewide coalition of immigrant rights groups, based in Miami, that runs a bilingual hotline, free citizenship clinics, and advocacy against state immigration enforcement laws.

  2. Solidarity SFLinstagram.com

    A grassroots organization building mutual aid networks and community power through direct action and solidarity initiatives across South Florida.

  3. Change Comes Nowchangecomesnowfl.org

    Florida nonprofit founded by Debra Bennett-Austin and Aniesha Austin that advocates for and supports incarcerated and formerly incarcerated women and girls.

  4. SURJ Floridainstagram.com

    Statewide Florida chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice, a network that organizes white people for racial and economic justice. Contact is by email, Instagram and Facebook.

  5. Florida Harm Reduction Initiativeinstagram.com

    Tallahassee harm reduction group, started in 2016, that gives out free naloxone, fentanyl test strips, syringes and safer-use kits and runs naloxone trainings.

  6. Coalition of Immokalee Workersciw-online.org

    Worker-based human rights organization internationally recognized for its achievements in fighting human trafficking and gender-based violence at work. Operating in Immokalee, FL.

  7. Florida Palestine Networkfloridapalestinenetwork.org

    Florida Palestine Network is a Palestinian woman founded and led organization struggling for the liberation of the Palestinian people and oppressed people everywhere.

  8. New Era Young Lords Floridainstagram.com

    Official Young Lords FL chapter ALL POWER TO THE PEOPLE
