Statewide Organizations15

  1. Carolina Abortion Fundcarolinaabortionfund.org

    Runs a toll-free helpline giving financial, practical and emotional support to people seeking abortions in North and South Carolina.

  2. Kirankiraninc.org

    A community-based organization providing crisis intervention, support services, and mutual aid to South Asian and Middle Eastern survivors of domestic violence across North Carolina. Through culturally-specific services, advocacy, and community organizing, they work to build safety and empowerment.

  3. NC Harm Reduction Coalitionnchrc.org

    Statewide harm reduction nonprofit running syringe services, overdose prevention, and Law Enforcement Assisted Diversion (LEAD) programs across North Carolina.

  4. NC Women's Prison Book Projectncwpb.org

    All-volunteer collective in Durham that mails books and letters to people in NC women's prisons and the Durham County Jail, about 15-25 packages a week.

  5. NC Treenctree.xyz

    A community-run calendar of DIY and radical events in North Carolina's Triangle area (Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill), where anyone can submit events.

  6. Operation Stop CPSoperationstopcps.com

    Durham group led by former CPS caseworker Amanda Wallace that campaigns against the child welfare system, offers education and family support, and pushes Durham County to fund family preservation.

  7. Cypress Fundcypressfund.org

    Funder that gives multi-year general operating grants to grassroots social justice groups led by affected communities in North and South Carolina, and organizes donors.

  8. Siembra NCsiembranc.org

    A Latinx organization providing mutual aid and organizing support to immigrant communities across North Carolina. They coordinate direct support through wage theft recovery, workers' rights education, and rapid response networks while building community power through solidarity initiatives and collective action.

  9. Triangle Copwatchinstagram.com

    A mutual aid collective organizing community defense and police accountability initiatives across North Carolina. They coordinate direct support, resource sharing, and community education while building networks of solidarity to protect communities and advocate for transformative justice solutions.

  10. Cooperate WNCcooperatewnc.org

    Western North Carolina nonprofit that convenes mutual aid and co-op projects, funds relief groups after Hurricane Helene, and trains local leaders in cooperative skills.

  11. ROAR (Rural Organizing and Resistance)instagram.com

    Hurricane Helene mutual aid hub based out of Western North Carolina.

  12. Holler Harm Reductionhollerharmreduction.org

    A free and confidential service that provides delivery of Harm Reduction supplies and overdose prevention materials to folks using drugs in Madison County, North Carolina.

  13. Down Home NCdownhomenc.org

    Rural membership organization with chapters in North Carolina small towns that organizes low-income and working-class people around local campaigns, voter registration, and issues like labor, food, water and land.

  14. Carolina Migrant Networkcarolinamigrantnetwork.org

    CMN offers free represent for people in NC/SC detention and removal proceedings.

  15. waywardinstagram.com

    Arts and politics collective that screens films, publishes zines and books, hosts tabletop gaming events, and offers short residencies focused on land care.
