  1. Tenants Union of Washington Statetenantsunion.org

    Grassroots organization building collective power through education, outreach, and direct action to win housing justice and tenant rights through a racial equity lens.

  2. Cascadia Food Programinstagram.com

    A mutual aid collective organizing food distribution and community support across Washington state. They coordinate food rescue, resource sharing, and direct support while building sustainable networks of food access and community care throughout the Cascadia region.

  3. SURJ Southwest Washingtonfacebook.com

    Local SURJ chapter organizing white people against white supremacy in the Lower Columbia and Southwest Washington region. Campaigns include Chinook Indian Nation federal recognition and ending an ICE contract in Cowlitz County.

  4. Khmer Anti-deportation and Advocacy Group (KhAAG)khaagwa.org

    The Khmer Advocacy and Advancement Group is a community-focused organization with a mission to empower and uplift the Khmer community. ​Our roots stem from advocating against increased deportations and raids by Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).

  5. Wasington Immigrant Solidarity Networkwaisn.org

    At WAISN, we are committed to defending immigrant and refugee communities from deportation while advocating for meaningful, systemic change.

  6. Zoned Out PNWzonedoutpnw.com

    Independent news project covering Washington state and the wider Pacific Northwest, with reporting on local issues such as surveillance cameras and utility rates.
