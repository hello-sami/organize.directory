Statewide Organizations14

  1. No More Deaths / No Más Muertesnomoredeaths.org

    Volunteer group in southern Arizona that leaves water and food for migrants in the desert, runs an aid camp near Arivaca, and documents abuses and deaths at the border.

  2. Alientoalientoaz.org

    Phoenix nonprofit led by undocumented and DACA youth. Runs education, arts and healing programs, a scholarship, and know-your-rights workshops for immigrant students and families.

  3. Trans Queer Pueblotqpueblo.org

    A safe space for LGBT+ migrants of color in Arizona.

  4. Ajo Samaritansajosamaritans.com

    Volunteer group in Ajo, Arizona that provides water and humanitarian aid in the borderlands and runs an aid office open Fridays and Saturdays.

  5. Mass Liberation Arizonamasslibaz.org

    Directly impacted people's organization in South Phoenix working to end mass incarceration in Arizona through organizing, police accountability, and decarceral legislation.

  6. Death Penalty Alternatives for Arizonaazdeathpenalty.org

    Statewide volunteer group that educates the public about the death penalty and campaigns for abolition and policy reform in Arizona, including documentary screenings and events.

  7. Equality Arizonaequalityarizona.org

    Arizona LGBTQ+ civil rights group that lobbies at the legislature, tracks anti-LGBTQ bills, and runs voter education and endorsements.

  8. Sonoran Prevention Worksspwaz.org

    Tempe-based harm reduction group doing street outreach, naloxone distribution, overdose prevention training, and HIV/HCV testing across Arizona, and advocating for drug policy reform.

  9. O'odham Piipaash Allianceinstagram.com

    An Indigenous-led collective fostering mutual aid and solidarity through cultural preservation and land defense in O'odham and Piipaash territories. They coordinate community support, traditional knowledge sharing, and solidarity networks while building power through Indigenous sovereignty and creating pathways for cultural resurgence.

  10. Save Oak Flatfacebook.com

    Apache-led group working to stop Resolution Copper's mine and protect Oak Flat, a sacred Apache site in Tonto National Forest, Arizona, through prayer runs, rallies and lawsuits.

  11. K'é Infoshopinstagram.com

    Anti-colonial, anti-heteropatriarchy, and anti-capitalist with indigenous feminism as our guiding principles. We're a collective of Diné uniting to liberate nihi k'ei/our relatives.

  12. AZ Comm-Unitydiscord.gg

    central hub for all people and groups within all of Arizona who are taking a stand against the rising wave of authoritarianism and fascism to come together to meet and coordinate with their local communities as well as communicate with the rest of the movement around the state.

  13. AZ50501 Discord Serverdiscord.gg

    Get the latest updates on the 50501 movement and connect with other Arizonans

  14. Haul Nohaulno.com

    A Diné-led group in northern Arizona that opposes uranium mining at the Pinyon Plain mine near the Grand Canyon and the hauling of its ore across Navajo and Havasupai land.
