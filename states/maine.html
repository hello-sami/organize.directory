    Maine harm reduction nonprofit and certified syringe service program run by people who use drugs. Provides syringes, naloxone, wound care, and peer navigation in Portland, Lewiston, and surrounding counties.

  3. Showing Up for Racial Justice - Midcoast SURJfacebook.com

    Midcoast Maine chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice, a network of white people organizing for racial justice. Part of the Mid-Coast Coalition for Justice.

  4. MaineTransNetmainetrans.net

    MaineTransNet is a community based organization led by transgender people for transgender people. We provide peer-to-peer support groups, social and community events, advocacy for the transgender community across Maine, and training for medical, mental health, and social service providers.

  5. Out in The Openweareoutintheopen.org

    Out in the Open connects rural LGBTQ+ people to build community, visibility, knowledge and power. We are a multiracial, majority working class, grassroots, movement and capacity building organization based in Wabanaki territory throughout the states of Vermont and Maine.

    Our chapter primarily organizes in the Portland Metropolitan area, although our campaigns and projects may operate statewide and our membership draws from every county in the state.
