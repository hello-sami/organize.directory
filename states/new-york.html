Statewide Organizations

  1. Prisoners' Legal Services of New York ↗ plsny.org

    Nonprofit law office providing free legal representation to people in New York State prisons, with offices in Albany, Buffalo, Ithaca, and Newburgh.

  2. F2L Network ↗ f2lrelieffund.org

    Sends commissary money and other support to incarcerated Black, Indigenous and people of color who are queer, trans or Two-Spirit in New York State prisons and NYC jails.

  3. Community Voices Heard (CVH) ↗ cvhaction.org

    Black- and brown-led membership organization in New York organizing low-income New Yorkers on housing, public housing preservation, and economic justice.

  4. New York Civil Liberties Union ↗ nyclu.org

    New York state affiliate of the ACLU that works on civil liberties through litigation, policy advocacy, and organizing.

  5. Envision Freedom Fund ↗ envisionfreedom.org

    Brooklyn-based fund that pays immigration bonds to free people from ICE detention in New York and helps them after release. Formerly the Brooklyn Community Bail Fund.

  6. Equality New York ↗ facebook.com

    Equality New York (EQNY) is a grassroots advocacy organization that advances the lives of all LGBTQI+ New Yorkers and their families.

  7. New York Immigration Coalition ↗ nyic.org

    Umbrella advocacy group of more than 200 immigrant and refugee organizations across New York State, working on immigrant rights, legal services, language access, and civic participation.

  8. Upstate NY Socialist Rifle Association ↗ linktr.ee

    We are the Upstate NY chapter of the Socialist Rifle Association.

  9. Upstate Anti-Fascist Coalition ↗ instagram.com

    Grassroots anti-fascist group based in Glens Falls, with members in Salem, South Glens Falls and Saratoga. Holds rallies and joins solidarity pickets with Migrant Justice.

  10. Northern New York Coalition ↗ instagram.com

    Massena-based coalition in New York's North Country that organizes protests and community events, including 2026 No Kings rallies in Massena and Ogdensburg.

  11. Housing Justice for All ↗ instagram.com

    Statewide coalition of over 80 tenant, homeless and public housing groups from Brooklyn to Buffalo, working on tenant protections, eviction limits and affordable housing.

  12. Release Aging People in Prison (RAPP) Campaign ↗ rappcampaign.com

    New York campaign working to release older people from state prison, including support for the Elder Parole bill. Organizes with local groups statewide.

  13. DecrimNY ↗ decrimny.com

    Coalition of sex workers, legal aid and advocacy groups working to decriminalize sex work in New York and backing the Stop Violence in the Sex Trades Act.

  14. Osborne Association ↗ osborneny.org

    Nonprofit serving people and families affected by incarceration in New York, with court advocacy, family programs, reentry services and housing, plus offices across the state.
