  1. NJ Trans and Queer Defenseinstagram.com

    Anarchist community self-defense project run by and for trans and queer people in New Jersey. Shares self-defense and safety resources; not membership-based.

  2. Say Gay NJinstagram.com

    Posts about school board meetings, actions and resources supporting LGBTQ+ youth and community in New Jersey. Focuses on school board policies and student rights.

  3. Books Behind Barsbooksbehindbarsnj.com

    Volunteer-run books-to-prisoners program based in Wildwood. Mails free books on request to people in New Jersey prisons and runs a pen pal program.

  4. New Jersey Harm Reduction Coalitionnjharmreduction.org

    A mutual aid network providing free harm reduction supplies and support across New Jersey. They coordinate direct assistance through naloxone distribution, syringe access, and safer use supplies while building community power through evidence-informed public health resources, education, and advocacy to promote the safety and dignity of people who use drugs.

  5. NJ Peace and Justice Worksnjpjw.org

    Coalition of survivors, families, faith groups and advocates pushing New Jersey to enforce its 2019 law limiting solitary confinement in state prisons.

  6. Ida's Bookshopx.com

    Black-owned independent bookstore at 734 Haddon Ave in Collingswood, NJ, named for Ida B. Wells and centered on women authors, artists and activists.

  7. New Jersey Red Umbrella Alliancenjrua.org

    The New Jersey Red Umbrella Alliance (NJRUA) is a working alliance of activists and allies who are dedicated to promoting, defending, and advocating for the human rights of sex workers in the state of New Jersey.

  8. New Jersey Abortion Access Fundnjaaf.org

    Volunteer-run fund that pays clinics to cover abortion costs for people traveling to, from and within New Jersey.

  9. Mutual Aid Morrismutualmorris.com

    Mutual aid and community building in Morris County, NJ

  10. Cosecha NJinstagram.com

    Fighting for Respect, Dignity and Permanent Protection for all.

  11. NJ Burninstagram.com

    New Jersey youth-led organization working to build sustained political struggle in our neighborhoods.
