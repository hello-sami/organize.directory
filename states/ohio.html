Statewide Organizations16

  1. Central Ohio Freedom Fundinstagram.com

    Community bail fund in Columbus and Franklin County, Ohio, started in 2020. Pays bail, advocates to end money bail, and runs a monthly commissary support program.

  2. Ohio Families Unite Against Police Brutalityofuapolicebrutality.com

    Ohio nonprofit founded by Sabrina Jordan that supports families of people killed by police and campaigns against police brutality through events and advocacy.

  3. Ohio Families Unite PACofupac.org

    Ohio group, the advocacy arm of Ohio Families Unite Against Police Brutality, that organizes around police violence, gun violence and the carceral system and publishes resources for survivors.

  4. SURJ Ohiofacebook.com

    Ohio hub of Showing Up for Racial Justice, a national network organizing white people against white supremacy. Connects local chapters such as Columbus, Northeast Ohio and Dayton.

  5. Trans Ohiotransohio.org

    TransOhio is dedicated to protecting and advancing the rights of trans, nonbinary, intersex, and gender nonconforming people in Ohio.

  6. Black Transmen of Ohiobtmohio.org

    Runs support groups, wellness retreats, and gender-affirming resources for Black transgender men and transmasculine and nonbinary people across Ohio.

  7. Black Queer & Intersectional Collectivebqic.net

    Black-led group in Columbus that organizes events and direct action for Black LGBTQIA+ people, runs an emergency fund for Black LGBTQIA+ Columbus residents, and helps run Columbus Community Pride.

  8. Trans Allies Ohiofacebook.com

    We are a grassroots coalition of transgender youth, their families, and allies who advocate for equality for Ohio's trans and non-binary people.

  9. Northeast Ohio Socialist Rifle Associationinstagram.com

    Northeast Ohio chapter of the Socialist Rifle Association. Offers firearms, medical and survival-skills training and mutual aid for working-class people.

  10. فكر بغيركinstagram.com
  11. Ohio Immigrant Allianceinstagram.com

    Cleveland-based group of immigrants and citizens working on immigrant rights in Ohio. Publishes research on immigration courts and runs the Ohio Is Home resource site and podcast.

  12. Central Ohio Revolutionary Socialists (CORS)instagram.com

    Membership-based socialist organization in and around Columbus, Ohio. Publishes The Columbus Worker and organizes on labor and campus issues.

  13. Central Ohio SRAinstagram.com

    Central Ohio chapter of the Socialist Rifle Association, a group that teaches firearm skills and community defense to working-class and marginalized people.

  14. Abortion Fund of Ohioabortionfundofohio.org

    Statewide fund that gives Ohioans financial help and practical support to get abortion care, and runs a legal information helpline.

  15. Ohio Divest Coalitionohiodivest.org

    Coalition of Ohio organizers and groups pushing public funds to divest from Israel Bonds. It reports over $27 million in divestment commitments, including $11 million from Franklin County.

  16. SURJ Northeast Ohiosurjneo.org

    Cleveland-based Northeast Ohio chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice, founded in 2015. It organizes white people against white supremacy through local campaigns in the Cleveland area and Nelsonville.
