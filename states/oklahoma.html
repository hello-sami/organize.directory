Statewide Organizations6

  1. Oklahomans for Equality (OKEQ)okeq.org

    A statewide organization seeking equal rights for 2SLGBTQIA+ individuals and families through intersectional advocacy, education, and mutual aid programs. They operate the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center in Tulsa, providing health services, counseling, community spaces, and direct support while building networks of care and solidarity across Oklahoma.

  2. Trans Advocacy Oklahomafacebook.com

    Grassroots group for trans, Two-Spirit, intersex and gender-diverse Oklahomans. It holds rallies and social events and gives direct aid through its partner, the Gender Umbrella Assistance Collective.

  3. Freedom Oklahomafreedomoklahoma.org

    Oklahoma City-based statewide group that advocates for 2SLGBTQ+ Oklahomans through education, HIV decriminalization work, youth programs, community organizing and resource referrals.

  4. Diversity Center of Oklahomadiversitycenterofoklahoma.org

    An LGBTQ+ and gender-diverse community center in Oklahoma City that offers behavioral health counseling and community resources, working out of the Expressions Community Center on NW 39th St.

  5. The Beacon Projectthebeaconproject.org

    A Norman nonprofit, formerly Pantry Partners, that gives high school teens facing hunger or homelessness free food, hygiene items, school supplies and tutoring.

  6. OKHRAokhra.net

    Oklahoma Harm Reduction Alliance, a grassroots harm reduction organization providing low barrier access to life saving supplies for people who use drugs (PWUD) in Oklahoma.
