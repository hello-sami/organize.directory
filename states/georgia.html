  1. Amplify GA Collaborative ↗ amplify-ga.org

  2. Asian American Advocacy Fund ↗ asianamericanadvocacyfund.org

    A Georgia 501(c)(4) political group based in Norcross that endorses candidates, runs multilingual voter outreach, and advocates for Asian American and Pacific Islander communities.

  3. Poder Unides ↗ poderunides.org

    Trans-led group in Atlanta that provides MARTA cards, interview clothing, job training, mentorship, mutual aid and counseling for trans, Black, Indigenous and Latinx people.

  4. Georgia Latino Alliance for Human Rights (GLAHR) ↗ glahr.org

    An Atlanta-based nonprofit, founded in 2001, that educates and organizes Latino immigrants across Georgia to defend their civil and human rights.
