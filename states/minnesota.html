Statewide Organizations23

  1. Anti-War Committeeantiwarcommittee.org

    All-volunteer group in Minneapolis that organizes protests and educational events against US war and militarism, including Palestine solidarity and divestment campaigns.

  2. DivestMNdivestmn.com

    Minnesota Anti-War Committee campaign site that tracks state pension investments tied to Israel and pushes the State Board of Investment to divest.

  3. Minnesota 8minnesota8.org

    We fight to end detention and deportation in Southeast Asian communities.

  4. Minnesota Workers Unitedinstagram.com

    A worker-led organization building solidarity and mutual aid networks across Minnesota.

  5. Rebuilding Together Minnesotartmn.org

    A mutual aid organization coordinating home repair and community revitalization initiatives of solidarity through volunteer mobilization and neighborhood preservation to ensure people can live independently and safely in their homes.

  6. Education Justice Minnesotainstagram.com

    A grassroots collective organizing mutual aid and solidarity initiatives focused on educational equity across Minnesota. They coordinate community resources, direct support, and advocacy while building networks of solidarity through student organizing, teacher support, and community education to ensure accessible and just education for all.

  7. Documenting MNlinktr.ee

    Community journalism project by and for Black youth in the Twin Cities. Documents and reports on police violence, state repression, and local community events.

  8. MN IWOC (Incarcerated Workers Organizing Committee)instagram.com

    The Incarcerated Workers Organizing Committee seeks to re-imagine a world where punishment and prisons aren't the only means of "justice" for society.

  9. Sahan Journalsahanjournal.com

    Nonprofit digital newsroom in St. Paul reporting on immigrants and communities of color in Minnesota. Founded in 2019.

  10. Minnesota Tool Librarymntoollibrary.org

    Volunteer-run nonprofit tool library with branches in Northeast Minneapolis and St. Paul, lending tools and offering shop space, classes, and Fix-It repair sessions.

  11. Minnesota Reformerminnesotareformer.com

    Independent nonprofit newsroom, part of States Newsroom, covering Minnesota state government, politics and policy.

  12. BLCK Pressblckpress.com

    Emmy-winning news and video production company in St. Paul, founded by journalist Georgia Fort, covering social justice and Black communities in Minnesota.

  13. Freedom Street Healthinstagram.com

    Community and street medic group in Minneapolis, founded in 2020. Provides medical support at protests and teaches CPR, Stop the Bleed and overdose response classes.

  14. REP (Relationships Evolving Possibilities)repformn.org

    Minneapolis network that trains community members to respond to crises without police. Runs community trainings and 'Radical Ecosystem Pods'; its emergency hotline has been paused since 2025.

  15. Black Visionsblvmn.org

    Black-led Minnesota organization working for Black liberation, centering Black, queer and trans people. Runs community organizing, membership programs and financial mutual aid in the Twin Cities area.

  16. New Justice Projectnewjusticeprojectmn.org

    Black-led Minnesota organization based in St. Paul that works on jobs, housing justice, community safety and building political power among Black Minnesotans.

  17. End Youth Prisons MNinstagram.com

    Youth-led campaign hosted by Minneapolis's Legal Rights Center that works to close youth prisons in Minnesota through organizing, art and legislative advocacy.

  18. Bdote Memory Mapbdotememorymap.org

    Interactive online map from the Minnesota Humanities Center and Allies: media/art, with Dakota people's video and audio stories about Bdote, the Twin Cities area.

  19. Makoce Ikikcupi (Land Recovery)makoceikikcupi.com

    Dakota-led reparative justice project that buys back land in Dakota homeland in Minnesota, with villages in Granite Falls and Mountain Lake, for food sovereignty and language revitalization.

  20. Minnesota Uprising Arrestee Supportinstagram.com

    MUAS is an ad-hoc grouping of arrestees, their friends and comrades, community members, activists, and legal workers.

  21. Minnesota Mutual Aid Eventsdiscord.gg

    Discord server for mutual aid events in the state.

  22. giniw collectiveginiw.org

    Indigenous women and two-spirit led land defense group in northern Minnesota. Formed in 2018 to fight the Line 3 pipeline; runs fellowships and Land Back work.

  23. Atlas Defenseatlasdefensemn.com

    Minneapolis training business offering firearms, de-escalation, community defense and workplace safety classes, aimed at BIPOC and LGBTQ+ communities.
