Statewide Organizations6

  1. Alternatives for Community and Environment (ACE)ace-ej.org

    Roxbury-based environmental justice group, founded in 1994, that runs youth organizing (REEP) and resident campaigns and gives legal and technical help to communities across Massachusetts.

  2. Queer & Trans Mutual Aid Massachusetts (QTMAMA)facebook.com

    Collective of LGBTQIA+ Massachusetts residents that organizes mutual aid, including small 'community pods' whose members help each other and bring larger needs to the wider group.

  3. Food For Freefoodforfree.org

    Cambridge-based food rescue nonprofit, founded in 1981, that collects surplus fresh food and distributes it through pantries, schools, and meal programs across Greater Boston.

  4. Lawrence Community Fridgelawrencefridge.neocities.org

    Free community fridge and free-goods table at 163 East Haverhill St., South Lawrence, MA, run on a take-what-you-need, give-what-you-can basis. Site is in English and Spanish.

  5. Stone Soup Cafethestonesoupcafe.org

    Pay-what-you-can community cafe at 399 Main St, Greenfield, MA. Runs soup nights, Saturday meals, a soup CSA, a community store and solidarity workshops.

  6. Massachussetts Transgender Political Coalitionmasstpc.org

    MTPC works to ensure the wellbeing, safety, and lived equity of all trans, nonbinary, and gender expansive community members in Massachusetts.
