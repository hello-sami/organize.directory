Statewide Organizations6

  1. Illinois Coalition to End Permanent Punishmentsendpermanentpunishments.org

    A coalition working to end permanent punishments and support formerly incarcerated individuals through advocacy, mutual aid, and community organizing. They focus on policy reform, community education, and direct support for impacted individuals and families across Illinois.

  2. Illinois Coalition Against Domestic Violenceilcadv.org

    Federally designated statewide coalition of more than 60 Illinois domestic violence service providers. It does policy advocacy, training and technical assistance.

  3. Illinois Single Payer Coalitionilsinglepayer.org

    Illinois advocacy group working for a publicly financed single-payer health care system, through public education and lobbying legislators. Based in Dolton, IL.

  4. Transgender Justice Law Projecttjlp.org

    We provide free, life and gender-affirming legal services to everyone on the gender spectrum regardless of financial status.

  5. Lift the Ban Coalitionltbcoalition.org

    Coalition of 40+ Chicago and Illinois tenant, labor, and community groups working to repeal the state's 1997 ban on rent control and win rent stabilization.

  6. Warehouse Workers For Justiceww4j.org

    A worker center founded in 2008 with offices in Chicago and Joliet that organizes Illinois warehouse workers for safer, better-paid jobs and recovers stolen wages.
