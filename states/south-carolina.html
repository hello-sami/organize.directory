Statewide Organizations

  1. Harriet Hancock LGBT Center ↗ harriethancockcenter.org

    Volunteer-staffed LGBTQ+ center in Columbia, SC. Runs social and peer-support programs and events, publishes a resource guide of affirming providers, and does advocacy work.

  2. The Ladies Room SC ↗ theladiesroomsc.org

    Black-led, trans-led LGBTQ+ center in Greenville, SC serving Black and Brown trans and nonbinary people with care navigation, HRT and name change help, housing and food assistance, and a weekly group.

  3. Carolina Abortion Fund ↗ carolinaabortionfund.org

    Nonprofit abortion fund with a toll-free helpline offering financial, practical, and emotional support to people seeking abortions in North and South Carolina.

  4. Cypress Fund ↗ cypressfund.org

    Social justice fund for North and South Carolina that gives grants to grassroots, BIPOC-led organizing groups and organizes donors. Runs the Rooted & Rise and Interdependence & Solidarity funds.

  5. Palmetto State Abortion Fund ↗ instagram.com

    All-volunteer abortion fund in South Carolina that pays clinics and covers travel, lodging, gas and food for people seeking abortions from SC, GA, AL and MS.

  6. Rainy Day Fund ↗ instagram.com

    Columbia, SC nonprofit that pays court costs and fees and coordinates legal help for people in South Carolina and Virginia whose main barrier is money.

  7. SC Housing Justice Network ↗ schjn.com

    Statewide coalition of working-class tenants in South Carolina organizing for affordable, safe housing. Has announced a rename to SC Tenant Union.

  8. South Carolinians for Alternatives to the Death Penalty (SCADP) ↗ scadp.org

    Greenville-based group working to abolish the death penalty in South Carolina through pen-pal programs, education events, community partnerships, and legislative advocacy.

  9. Carolina Migrant Network ↗ carolinamigrantnetwork.org

    CMN offers free represent for people in NC/SC detention and removal proceedings.
