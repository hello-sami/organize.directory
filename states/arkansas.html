Statewide Initiatives11

  1. Arkansas Community Advocatesinstagram.com

    A grassroots collective building mutual aid networks and community power across Arkansas. They coordinate direct support, resource sharing, and solidarity initiatives while advocating for social justice and community care.

  2. Arkansas Justice Reform Coalitionarjusticereform.org

    Fayetteville-based nonprofit working to end mass incarceration. Runs court monitoring, a legal clinic, parole support groups, and the Hummingbird Project bail fund.

  3. Taste of Olam Habainstagram.com

    Jewish, queer, disabled, feminist collective in Little Rock, Arkansas, that hosts holiday and community events for Jewish people in Central Arkansas.

  4. Arkansas Community Organizationarkansascommunity.org

    A membership group of low- and moderate-income Arkansans in Little Rock and Pine Bluff that campaigns on healthcare, housing, and wages, and offers eviction defense and free tax prep.

  5. Arkansas Disability Coalitionardisabilitycoalition.org

    Statewide nonprofit in Arkansas giving information, peer support and health-care navigation to people with disabilities and families of children with special health care needs.

  6. Arkansas Abortion Support Networkarabortionsupport.org

    Little Rock nonprofit that funds abortion care for Arkansans traveling out of state, gives out free emergency contraception, and runs the Y.O.U. Center resource center.

  7. Arkansas RAPPSarrapps.org

    Little Rock nonprofit working to end new HIV infections in Arkansas, offering PrEP, at-home testing, telehealth and outreach on sexual health and harm reduction.

  8. Intransitiveintransitive.org

    Trans-led advocacy group in Arkansas offering voting guidance, protest-rights education and a Migra Watch program for trans Arkansans.

  9. Queer Collective of Central Arkansasinstagram.com

    Queer families and individuals in Central Arkansas gather through potlucks, support groups, game nights and other social events. Membership is by application; based in Little Rock.

  10. Legal Aid of Arkansasarlegalaid.org

    A non-profit organization providing free legal services to low-income Arkansans. They offer legal assistance in civil matters including housing, family law, consumer protection, and public benefits, while working to ensure equal access to justice through advocacy and community education.

  11. DecARceratedecarceratear.org

    Working to affirm human dignity by confronting unjust systems.
