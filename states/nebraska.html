Statewide Organizations6

  1. DSA Nebraskadsanebraska.org

    The Lincoln chapter of Democratic Socialists of America, which also organizes at-large DSA members across Nebraska. It holds general meetings, brake light clinics, socials and book clubs.

  2. OutNebraskaoutnebraska.org

    Statewide LGBTQ+ nonprofit based in Lincoln that tracks and lobbies on Nebraska legislation, shares voter resources, and runs the annual Prairie Pride Film Festival.

  3. Nebraska Appleseedneappleseed.org

    Nebraska nonprofit law and policy advocacy group working on immigrant rights, food access, health care and housing, including litigation and ballot campaigns.

  4. Nebraska AIDS Projectnap.org

    Nonprofit founded in 1984 offering free HIV testing, PrEP access, case management, a food pantry, and support groups, with offices in Omaha, Lincoln, Norfolk, Kearney, and Scottsbluff.

  5. Norfolk Area Pridenorfolkareapride.org

    Nonprofit serving LGBTQ+ people in Northeast Nebraska. Runs an annual Pride festival in Norfolk and offers community resources, education and gender-affirming clothing assistance.

  6. Nebraska Abortion Resourcesneabortionresources.org

    Nebraska's statewide abortion fund, covering procedure costs plus travel, lodging and childcare, and giving out free emergency contraception kits through its Speak E.C. program.
