Statewide Organizations

  1. Hearts on a Wire ↗ heartsonawire.org

    Philadelphia collective supporting incarcerated transgender and nonbinary people in Pennsylvania prisons through correspondence and community organizing. Holds open weekly meetings.

  2. Let's Get Free: The Women and Trans Prisoner Defense Committee ↗ letsgetfree.info

    Pittsburgh-based group led by incarcerated women and trans people. Campaigns for commutation reform and against life sentences, and runs Virtual Healing therapy and Daughters Magazine.

  3. Pennsylvania Institutional Law Project (PILP) ↗ pilp.org

    Free legal help and litigation for people incarcerated in Pennsylvania, focused on constitutional and civil rights. Has offices in the eastern, central and western parts of the state.

  4. Commutation in PA Now! ↗ commutationnow.com

    Pennsylvania campaign pushing the state Board of Pardons to be more transparent, including keeping cameras on during online clemency and commutation hearings.

  5. Montgomery County Food Not Bombs ↗ instagram.com

    Food Not Bombs chapter in Montgomery County, PA, started by Upper Dublin high school students in 2021. Serves free vegetarian meals and groceries in Norristown and Pottstown.

  6. Eastern PA Trans Equity Project ↗ patransequity.org

    EPTEP provides direct services to transgender and gender-diverse Pennsylvanians in a thirty-three county region in Eastern PA.

  7. Trans Minors Rights ↗ transminorsrights.org

    Our goal is to empower transgender youth by educating our communities on issues facing young transgender people and providing youth with the knowledge and tools they need to combat gender dysphoria. Operating out of Lancaster.

  8. TransFamily of NW Pennsylvania ↗ transfamilynwpa.org

    TransFamily of NWPA is an organization created to serve the transgender community of Northwestern Pennsylvania serving Erie, Crawford, and Warren counties.
