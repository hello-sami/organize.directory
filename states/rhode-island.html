  1. Providence, Rhode Island

Statewide Organizations8

  1. SURJ Rhode Islandinstagram.com

    The Rhode Island chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice. It organizes white people to act on racial justice and runs a childcare collective for organizing led by people of color.

  2. George Wiley Centergeorgewileycenter.org

    A grassroots organization in Pawtucket working for social and economic justice across Rhode Island. Through mutual aid and direct action, they organize around utility justice, housing security, and food security, providing weekly rights clinics and community support for those facing utility shutoffs or eviction.

  3. Rhode Island Coalition to End Homelessnessrihomeless.org

    A statewide coalition working collaboratively with advocates, providers, and faith-based organizations to prevent and end homelessness. They provide direct support through their 24/7 Coordinated Entry help line, manage housing placement services, and successfully advocated for the country's first Homeless Bill of Rights.

  4. Alliance of Rhode Island Southeast Asians for Education (ARISE)ariseducation.org

    A youth-led organization combining leadership development with community organizing to mobilize Southeast Asian and other Rhode Island youth of color. Through mutual aid and education justice initiatives, ARISE works to support student activists and create systemic change in Providence schools.

  5. Alliance to Mobilize Our Resistance (AMOR)amorri.org

    Providence, RI network that runs an immigration defense hotline and case management, and organizes against detention, deportation and police violence.

  6. Ocean State Advocacyoceanstateadvocacy.org

    Sex worker-led group in Rhode Island, started in 2020. Runs a mutual aid fund for local sex workers, does community research, and runs the Harm Reduxx PVD harm reduction project.

  7. TGI Network of Rhode Islandtginetwork.org

    Serving the needs of the Transgender, Gender diverse, and Intersex (TGI) communities in Rhode Island and surrounding areas through support, advocacy, and education.

  8. Call Off Your Old Tired Ethics (COYOTE)coyoteri.org

    A sex worker rights organization based in Rhode Island that advocates for the health, safety, and human rights of sex workers through education, advocacy, and community support.
