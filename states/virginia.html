Statewide Organizations11

  1. Blue Ridge Community Bail Fundblueridgebailfund.org

    A volunteer-run mutual aid organization providing bail assistance to people held pre-trial in the Shenandoah Valley region, including Staunton, Harrisonburg, Page County, and Charlottesville. Through their revolving bail fund, they work to ensure that people do not remain incarcerated simply due to inability to pay, while addressing the disproportionate impact of cash bail on communities of color and low-income individuals.

  2. Power Safe Place Resource Center of Virginiapowersafeplace.org

    Front Royal, VA nonprofit for LGBTQ+ people and people of color offering HIV testing, harm reduction, emergency hotel vouchers, food, clothing, transportation and job readiness help.

  3. Transgender Assistance Program of Virginiainstagram.com

    A 501(c)3 non-profit organization by trans people, for trans people. Dedicated to ending trans homelessness across the Commonwealth of VA.

  4. SURJ Historic Trianglesurj-ht.wixsite.com

    The Williamsburg-area chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice. It organizes white people against racism through workshops, book studies and film screenings in Williamsburg, James City County and York County.

  5. SURJ Northern Virginiasurjnova.org

    Northern Virginia chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice, organizing white people for racial justice through education, mobilizing, and campaigns.

  6. The 40 Strongthe40strong.com

    Founded in 2021 by Virginia DOC prisoner Courtney Henson, 40 Strong sends newsletters to incarcerated members, advocates for prison reform and shares reentry resources.

  7. Blue Ridge Abortion Fundblueridgeabortionfund.org

    Charlottesville-based fund, started in 1989, that helps people in Virginia pay for abortions and covers transportation and lodging.

  8. Beloved Community Incubatorbelovedcommunityincubator.org

    Helps workers in the DC, Maryland, and Virginia region start worker-owned co-ops, and runs the DC Solidarity Economy Loan Fund.

  9. The Care Collective of SWVAthecarecollectiveofswva.org

    Southwest Virginia group, based in Duffield, that offers a therapy clinic, a community apothecary, and grief support circles.

  10. The Green Cabthegreencab.org

    Our mission is to provide a range of practical and logistical support options for people seeking abortion care in Virginia.

  11. Rainy Day Fundrainydayfund.us

    Gives money for housing, ID, court costs and other immediate needs to people involved with or at risk of legal system involvement in South Carolina and Virginia. Requests come through a social worker, case manager or attorney.
