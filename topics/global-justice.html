Global Justice & International Solidarity

3 groups

Mutual aid networks and initiatives focused on global justice, anti-imperialism, and international solidarity.

International Networks

  1. International League of Peoples' Struggle (ILPS) ↗ peoplesstruggle.org

    International alliance of anti-imperialist mass organizations that publishes statements and coordinates campaigns on war, repression, and people's movements worldwide.

  2. Syrian American Medical Society (SAMS) ↗ sams-usa.net

    A global medical relief organization providing crucial healthcare services and organizing mutual aid networks in conflict zones and underserved regions. They coordinate medical missions, emergency response, and direct support while building sustainable healthcare infrastructure and training local medical professionals.

  3. Grassroots Global Justice Alliance ↗ ggjalliance.org

    National alliance of over 60 US grassroots organizing groups that links them with global social movements on climate justice, gender justice, anti-war work and a just transition.
