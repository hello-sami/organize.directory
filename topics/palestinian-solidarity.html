Palestinian Solidarity

25 groups

National Initiatives25

  1. Palestinian Youth Movementpalestinianyouthmovement.com

    Transnational, independent, grassroots movement of young Palestinians organizing for the liberation of Palestine and the return of all Palestinian refugees.

  2. International Solidarity Movement (ISM)palsolidarity.org

    A Palestinian-led movement committed to resisting the Israeli occupation of Palestinian land using nonviolent, direct-action methods and principles. ISM provides on-the-ground international presence and witnesses human rights violations while supporting Palestinian-led nonviolent resistance.

  3. US Campaign for Palestinian Rights (USCPR)uscpr.org

    National coalition supporting the Palestine solidarity movement, providing resources and strategy to local organizers. Formerly the US Campaign to End the Israeli Occupation.

  4. Mennonite Actionmennoniteaction.org

    A movement of Mennonites organizing mutual aid and direct action for Palestine solidarity. They coordinate resources, organize nonviolent actions, and build interfaith coalitions while providing material support and advocating for peace and justice through their faith-based approach.

  5. Friends of Sabeel North America (FOSNA)fosna.org

    An interdenominational Christian organization providing mutual aid and solidarity through education, advocacy, and nonviolent action for Palestine. They amplify Palestinian voices and coordinate support through church networks, community organizing, and direct action campaigns.

  6. Adalah Justice Projectadalahjusticeproject.org

    Palestinian Advocacy Organization based in the U.S., aiming to shift policy & public discourse on Palestine + Organizing for collective liberation

  7. Friends of Palestinian Medical Relief Society (FPMRS)friendsofpmrs.org

    US nonprofit, founded in Atlanta, that raises money for the Palestinian Medical Relief Society's clinics, mobile clinics, and emergency care in Gaza and the West Bank.

  8. CPT Palestineinstagram.com

    Community Peacemaker Teams' Palestine program, based in Al-Khalil/Hebron. It accompanies Palestinians, monitors checkpoints, documents abuses, and supports nonviolent resistance to the occupation.

  9. International Jewish Anti-Zionist Network (IJAN)ijan.org

    International network of Jewish antizionist activists with US and other national chapters, running campaigns such as Stop the Jewish National Fund.

  10. Rebuilding Alliancerebuildingalliance.org

    Redwood City, CA nonprofit supporting Palestinian families with aid, construction and education projects, mainly in Gaza, and advocating for Palestinian rights.

  11. BDS Movementbdsmovement.net

    Palestinian-led global campaign, coordinated by the Palestinian BDS National Committee, calling for boycotts, divestment and sanctions against Israel.

  12. Decolonize This Placex.com

    New York City activist and art collective that organizes around Palestine, Indigenous struggle, Black liberation, wage workers and de-gentrification. Known for the 2019 Whitney Museum protests.

  13. Refuser Solidarity Networkrefuser.org

    Nonprofit founded in 2003 that supports Israeli war resisters and conscientious objectors, including legal fees, mental health support and press campaigns for refusing youth and soldiers.

  14. Palestinian Feminist Collectivepalestinianfeministcollective.org

    Collective of Palestinian and Arab feminists that publishes reports and resources on gendered and colonial violence, including the 'A Predatory State' report with Progressive International.

  15. Al-Awda: The Palestine Right to Return Coalitional-awdapalestine.org

    Coalition based in Coral Springs, Florida that advocates for Palestinian refugees' right of return, holds demonstrations and cultural programs, and runs a refugee education fund.

  16. BADIL Resource Center for Palestinian Residency and Refugee Rightsbadil.org

    Bethlehem-based human rights organization working on Palestinian refugee and residency rights through legal advocacy, community and international mobilization, and training programs.

  17. Al-Shabaka: The Palestinian Policy Networkal-shabaka.org

    Global network of Palestinian analysts that publishes policy briefs, commentaries, and roundtables on Palestinian politics, economics, civil society, and refugees.

  18. Samidoun: Palestinian Prisoner Solidarity Networksamidoun.net

    international network of organizers and activists working to build solidarity with Palestinian prisoners in their struggle for freedom.

  19. Theater Workers for a Ceasefiretheaterworkersforaceasefire.com

    US-based theater workers demand a ceasefire in Palestine.

  20. Writers Against the War on Gazawritersagainstthewarongaza.com

    Building a cultural front for a free Palestine. They write and publish the New York War Crimes.

  21. Pal Shieldpalshield.org

    American - Palestinian led organization providing life-saving supplies to protect Palestinian farmers and communities.

  22. Jewish Voice for Peacejewishvoiceforpeace.org

    National Jewish organization based in Berkeley, CA that organizes for Palestinian liberation, supports BDS, and runs campaigns and local chapters across the US.

  23. Global Sumud Flotillainstagram.com

    International civilian flotilla that sails boats with aid to Gaza to challenge the blockade. Its 2026 mission set out in April from several countries and was intercepted by Israeli forces.

  24. National SJPinstagram.com

    Student-run network that supports Students for Justice in Palestine chapters on North American campuses, with campaigns, statements and an annual national conference.

  25. Artists Against Apartheidinstagram.com

    Network of artists and cultural workers, with 15,000+ signatories, who make and share art and posters in support of Palestinian liberation.
