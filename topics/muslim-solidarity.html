Muslim Solidarity

8 groups

National Initiatives

  1. Muslims for Just Futures ↗ muslimsforjustfutures.org

    Muslims for Just Futures (MJF) is a grassroots organization that builds power in Muslim communities through collective care, organizing, advocacy, and movement-building.

  2. Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR) ↗ cair.com

    National Muslim civil rights and advocacy group with state chapters. It handles discrimination complaints and legal cases, tracks anti-Muslim incidents, and lobbies on policy.

  3. MPower Change ↗ mpowerchange.org

    Muslim-led digital advocacy group, founded in 2016, that runs national campaigns against Islamophobia and surveillance, plus voter engagement (#MyMuslimVote) and Palestine solidarity work.

  4. Muslim Advocates ↗ muslimadvocates.org

    National Muslim civil rights organization based in Washington, DC. It files lawsuits and FOIA requests and publishes know-your-rights resources.

  5. Muslim Legal Fund of America (MLFA) ↗ mlfa.org

    Texas-based nonprofit that pays for legal defense of Muslims in the US in criminal, civil and immigration cases, and runs community education on legal rights.

  6. ICNA Relief ↗ icnarelief.org

    Muslim charity in the US run by ICNA. It runs food pantries, transitional housing, family services, health services and disaster relief.

  7. Muslim Anti-Racism Collaborative (MuslimARC) ↗ muslimarc.org

    Nonprofit that makes anti-racism curriculum and trainings for Muslim communities and institutions. Mailing address is in Alta Loma, CA.

  8. Muslim Justice League ↗ muslimjusticeleague.org

    Boston-based group working on Muslim civil rights, including campaigns against surveillance technology, countering violent extremism programs, and police abuse.
