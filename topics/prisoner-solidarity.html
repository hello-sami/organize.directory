Prisoner Solidarity

18 groups

Organizations and resources supporting incarcerated individuals, their families, and communities through mutual aid, advocacy, education, and re-entry assistance.

Re-Entry Support1

  1. The National Reentry Networkthenationalreentrynetwork.org

    Washington, DC nonprofit for people returning from incarceration, offering peer navigation, job training (READY4WORK), professional clothing and health support.

National Initiatives7

  1. Abolitionist Law Centerabolitionistlawcenter.org

    Public interest law firm and grassroots organization fighting for the rights of people in prison through litigation and community organizing.

  2. Legal Services for Prisoners with Children (LSPC)prisonerswithchildren.org

    Grassroots civil and human rights organization building public awareness of structural racism in the criminal legal system while organizing communities impacted by incarceration through legal advocacy, policy work, and the All of Us or None movement.

  3. Prison Law Officeprisonlaw.com

    Legal advocacy organization protecting the constitutional rights of incarcerated people through litigation, education, and advocacy, with significant victories improving prison conditions and healthcare access in California and Arizona.

  4. Prison Activist Resource Center (PARC)prisonactivist.org

    Prison abolitionist group providing free resources and support to incarcerated people through their comprehensive national directory of organizations, while working to expose and challenge systemic oppression in the Prison Industrial Complex.

  5. Incarcerated Workers Organizing Committee (IWOC)incarceratedworkers.org

    A prisoner-led section of the Industrial Workers of the World coordinating mutual aid and labor organizing inside prisons. They organize direct support through solidarity campaigns, resource sharing, and advocacy while building networks between incarcerated workers and outside supporters to fight prison slavery and the prison industrial complex.

  6. Alyssa Rodriguez Center for Gender Justicearcgenderjustice.org

    A group in Pennsylvania, Florida, New York and Georgia that connects incarcerated survivors and jailhouse lawyers and makes advocacy materials against gender-based violence in prisons.

  7. Free Casey Nowfreecaseynow.noblogs.org

    A grassroots campaign fostering mutual aid and solidarity through support for Casey Goonan

Books & Education2

  1. Prison Library Support Network (PLSN)plsn.org

    NYC collective of librarians and activists, founded in 2015, that answers information requests by mail from incarcerated people and supports library services in prisons.

  2. LGBT Books to Prisonerslgbtbookstoprisoners.org

    A volunteer-run group in Madison, Wisconsin, that mails free books to incarcerated LGBTQ people across the US. Founded in 2006.

Updates from Inside8

  1. Prison Legal News (PLN)prisonlegalnews.org

    Monthly publication covering criminal justice issues and prison conditions through investigative reporting, while providing legal resources and analysis to incarcerated people. Published by the Human Rights Defense Center with a focus on amplifying voices from inside.

  2. Between the Barsbetweenthebars.org

    Blog platform where people in US prisons publish letters and writing. Handwritten letters are scanned and posted, and readers can comment. Hosted by MIT's Center for Civic Media.

  3. Belly Zineinstagram.com

    Abolitionist journal and magazine of essays, letters, poems and art from incarcerated people and outside supporters. Free to people in prison. Started in 2020 as 'In the Belly'.

  4. Free Casey Nowinstagram.com

    Casey is a dedicated community educator, writer, distroist, and printer who has committed their life to struggles for liberation. A federal investigation by the FBI and several other law enforcement agencies resulted in Casey’s arrest for alleged politically motivated “crimes.” Since June 17 2024, Casey has been incarcerated at Santa Rita Jail facing federal felonies that could result in up to 20 years of imprisonment.

  5. FREE PALESTINE 🇵🇸❤️‍🔥instagram.com
  6. Free Alex Stokes Projectinstagram.com

    Campaign supporting Alex Stokes, an Albany, NY journalist and anti-fascist serving a 20-year sentence for a 2021 clash with Proud Boys. Pushes for clemency and letter writing.

  7. Justice for Keith LaMarinstagram.com

    Grassroots campaign for Keith LaMar, an Ohio death row prisoner held in long-term solitary who says he is innocent of a killing in the 1993 Lucasville uprising. Shares case updates and his writing.

  8. Stand with Tarek Bazrouk 🇵🇸instagram.com
