Legal Support & Advocacy4

  1. Al Otro Ladoalotrolado.org

    Legal and humanitarian aid for refugees, deportees, and other migrants in the US and Tijuana, based in San Ysidro, CA.

  2. Justice Action Centerjusticeactioncenter.org

    Legal advocacy group that brings federal impact lawsuits and uses storytelling to defend immigrant rights, including DACA, humanitarian parole, and ICE enforcement in sensitive locations.

  3. Immigrant Defense Projectimmigrantdefenseproject.org

    Providing legal resources, fighting unjust laws, supporting detained individuals, and advocating for fair immigration policies.

  4. Immigrant Legal Resource Centerilrc.org

    Working with and educating immigrants, community organizations, and the legal sector to help build a democratic society that values diversity and the rights of all people.

National Networks & Coalitions5

  1. National Network for Immigrant and Refugee Rights (NNIRR)nnirr.org

    National network founded in 1986 that works on immigrant and refugee rights. Does human rights training, popular education and border justice advocacy, and builds alliances between grassroots groups.

  2. National Day Laborer Organizing Networkndlon.org

    NDLON improves the lives of day laborers, migrants and low-wage workers. We build leadership and power among those facing injustice so they can challenge inequality and expand labor, civil and political rights for all.

  3. Detention Watch Networkdetentionwatchnetwork.org

    Detention Watch Network is a national membership organization that brings together advocates from diverse backgrounds to unify strategy, build partnerships and strengthen the movement to end immigration detention.

  4. Southeast Asian Freedom Networkseafn.org

    National network of Southeast Asian grassroots groups, formed in 2002, that organizes against deportation and criminalization of Southeast Asian refugee communities.

  5. Stop ICE Mobile Alertsstopice.net

    Nationwide text-message alert system where people report and receive alerts about ICE activity in their area, with a live map and license plate lookup. No app required.

  1. ABISAabisa.org

    Detroit nonprofit that supports Black and African immigrants and refugees through social and legal services, advocacy and community resources.
