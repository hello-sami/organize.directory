Trans Support6

  1. Transgender, Gender-Variant & Intersex Justice Project (TGIJP)tgijp.org

    A San Francisco nonprofit led by Black trans people that supports transgender, gender-variant and intersex people inside and coming out of prison, with re-entry, housing and legal advocacy programs.

  2. Trans Asylum Seeker Support Networkinstagram.com

    A national mutual aid collective building an autonomous organization to provide very longterm, comprehensive, and needs-based support to trans women and LGBTQI+ people that is not available through nonprofits, churches, or sponsorships.

  3. Trans Doe Task Forcetransdoetaskforce.org

    A trans-led nonprofit organization that finds and researches cases of LGBTQ+ missing and murdered persons.

  4. Point of Pridepointofpride.org

    Nonprofit that funds gender-affirming care for trans people, including surgery grants, 18 months of free HRT care, hair removal, small grants, and free binders.

  5. 360 Health X360healthx.org

    Nonprofit led by trans people of color offering gender-affirming health services, support groups, fitness, insurance navigation and free binders and clothing in VA, DC, MD and NY, plus virtual services.

  6. Gender Liberation Movementinstagram.com

    Building a movement for bodily autonomy, self-determination, collectivism, & fulfillment. It’s our bodies, our genders, our choices, our futures!

Legal Advocacy & Resources3

  1. ACLU LGBTQ Rights Legislative Trackeraclu.org

    A comprehensive resource tracking anti-LGBTQ legislation across U.S. state legislatures, maintained by the ACLU's legal and advocacy team. The tracker monitors bills targeting transgender rights, local protections, and religious exemptions while providing updates on the status of legislation and related court cases. This tool helps communities stay informed about legislative threats and organize mutual aid responses to support affected LGBTQ+ individuals.

  2. LGBTQ Freedom Fundlgbtqfund.org

    Pays immigration bond for LGBTQ people in ICE detention in every US state, and pretrial bail for LGBTQ people in Florida jails.

  3. National Center for LGBTQ Rights (NCLR)nclrights.org

    Legal organization that brings impact litigation and does policy work and education on LGBTQ civil rights, including youth, family, immigration, and discrimination cases. Works across the US.

Community Networks & Libraries2

  1. Queer Liberation Libraryqueerliberationlibrary.org

    Online library of LGBTQ+ books and resources, with digital collections and memberships. Fiscally sponsored by a Massachusetts nonprofit and serves people across the US.

  2. LGBTQ+ Mutual Aid Networkfacebook.com

    An online community fostering mutual aid and solidarity through direct support and resource sharing for LGBTQ+ individuals. They coordinate assistance requests, community support, and solidarity networks while building power through digital organizing, peer support, and creating virtual spaces for LGBTQ+ mutual aid.

Intersex Support1

  1. Intersex Justice Projectintersexjusticeproject.org

    Intersex Justice Project works to end invasive and unnecessary surgeries on intersex youth by empowering intersex people of color as change-makers.

Black LGBTQ Support1

  1. Counter Narrative Project (CNP)thecounternarrative.org

    Atlanta-based nonprofit that works to change how the media portrays Black gay, bisexual, and queer men. Runs The Reckoning publication, a podcast network, media grants, and a leadership institute.
