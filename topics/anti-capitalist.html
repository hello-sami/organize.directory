Anti-Capitalist Organizations & Resources

4 groups

  1. Union for Radical Political Economics (URPE)urpe.org

    Membership organization of academics and activists, founded in 1968, that studies radical political economy. Publishes the Review of Radical Political Economics and runs conference panels, caucuses and webinars.

  2. Counter Currentscountercurrents.org

    Reader-funded independent news site based in Kottayam district, Kerala, India. Publishes left-leaning articles on politics, movements and the environment, mostly about India.

  3. Green and Red Podcastgreenandredpodcast.org

    Podcast hosted by Bob Buzzanco and Scott Parkin. Interviews organizers, academics and artists on radical environmental, labor and anti-capitalist politics.

  4. CounterVortexcountervortex.org

    Independent news and commentary site run by Bill Weinberg in New York City. Publishes daily reports, features and podcasts on global politics, conflict and social movements.
