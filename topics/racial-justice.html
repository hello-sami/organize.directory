Racial Justice Resources & Support

Organizations and resources focused on racial equity, anti-racism, and supporting communities of color through mutual aid, solidarity, and collective action.

National Organizations10

  1. Spirit of Mandelaspiritofmandela.org

    Coalition founded in 2018 by Black liberation organizers and former political prisoners. It documents US human rights abuses, held a 2021 international tribunal, and runs the Peoples' Senate network.

  2. Showing Up for Racial Justice (SURJ)surj.org

    National network of white people organizing for racial and economic justice through local chapters, campaigns, trainings and webinars.

  3. Catalyst Projectcollectiveliberation.org

    Trains and mentors white activists and majority-white groups to do anti-racist organizing, including through its Anne Braden Program. Started in the Bay Area.

  4. White Coats 4 Black Liveswhitecoats4blacklives.org

    A national network of medical students and healthcare workers, with chapters at health-sciences schools, that organizes against racism in medicine and healthcare.

  5. Black Alliance for Peaceblackallianceforpeace.com

    Anti-war, anti-imperialist organization rooted in the Black radical tradition. It campaigns against US militarization, including AFRICOM, and publishes news bulletins.

  6. Presente.orgpresente.org

    National online organizing group for Latinx communities. Runs petitions and campaigns on immigration, climate, policing, and demilitarization of the border.

  7. American-Arab Anti-Discrimination Committee (ADC)adc.org

    National Arab American civil rights organization based in Washington, DC. Provides legal assistance and advocates against discrimination.

  8. National Alliance Against Racist & Political Repression (NAARPR)naarpr.org

    Founded in 1973 in Chicago to mount organized action against unjust treatment of individuals because of race or political beliefs. Has multiple chapters across the US.

  9. BYP100byp100.org

    BYP100 is National, member-based organization of Black 18-35 year old activists and organizers, dedicated to creating justice and freedom for all Black people. We do this through building a network focused on transformative leadership development, direct action organizing, advocacy, and political education using a Black queer feminist lens.

  10. Turpin Caresturpincares.org

    Runs a twice-monthly free store, a pantry box and care packages with food and hygiene supplies for people in need in Beverly/Morgan Park on Chicago's south side.
