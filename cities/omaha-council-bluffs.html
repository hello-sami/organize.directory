Omaha / Council Bluffs

9 groups · Nebraska

  1. Strongly Worded Letters ↗ stronglywordedomaha.org

    UNO alumni-led volunteer group in Omaha that explains local government and gives residents scripts for contacting officials. Offers office hours and city budget workshops.

  2. Urban Abbey ↗ theurbanabbey.org

    Nonprofit coffee shop, bookstore and United Methodist church at 1026 Jackson St in Omaha's Old Market. Hosts worship, a campus ministry for queer students, and community events.

  3. PFLAG Omaha ↗ pflag-omaha.org

    The Omaha chapter of PFLAG, founded in 1984. It holds monthly peer-support meetings for LGBTQ+ people, their families, and allies at Countryside Community Church.

  4. Together Omaha ↗ togetheromaha.org

    Omaha nonprofit that runs food pantries and homelessness and housing programs, and does policy advocacy on food and housing. Based at 812 S 24th St.

  5. Inclusive Communities ↗ inclusive-communities.org

    Omaha nonprofit that runs anti-bias education and leadership programs for schools, businesses, nonprofits and government agencies in Nebraska and Iowa.

  6. Omaha DSA ↗ omahadsa.org

    Omaha chapter of the Democratic Socialists of America, organizing in eastern Nebraska and western Iowa on pro-union work, letter campaigns, and events.

  7. I Be Black Girl ↗ ibbg.org

    Omaha organization for Black women, femmes and girls, running community-led investments, economic opportunity, maternal health (doula training) and people power programs.

  8. Omaha Food Not Bombs ↗ omaha.foodnotbombs.us

    Since 1996 Food Not Bombs Omaha has been distributing food free of charge on the corner of 14th and Farnam at 2pm every Sunday.

  9. /r/OmahaMutualAid ↗ reddit.com

    Subreddit for Omaha Mutual Aid, a group that shares mutual aid resources and holds biweekly in-person peer support meetings at the downtown Omaha library.
