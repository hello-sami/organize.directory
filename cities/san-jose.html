San Jose

8 groups · California

  1. San Jose Community Fridge ↗ instagram.com

    Community fridge and pantry shelf at Paper Moon Cafe in downtown San Jose where anyone can take free food. Run on donations and volunteers.

  2. San Jose Mutual Aid ↗ instagram.com

    A grassroots organization fostering mutual aid and community support in San Jose. They coordinate direct assistance, resource sharing, and solidarity networks while building power through community organizing and creating spaces for collective care.

  3. San Jose Peace & Justice Center ↗ sanjosepeace.org

    Resource center and community space in downtown San Jose that provides free meeting space, printing and audio equipment and hosts events for peace and social justice causes.

  4. San Jose Food Not Bombs ↗ sjfoodnotbombs.netlify.app

    Shares free vegan food, fresh produce, hygiene supplies and clothing, no questions asked, every Sunday afternoon at St. James Park in downtown San Jose.

  5. Youth Space ↗ youthspace.org

    LGBTQ+ drop-in center for ages 13-25 at 2635 Zanker Rd in San Jose, run by Caminar. Offers support groups, art workshops, snacks and therapy.

  6. Community Got Us ↗ instagram.com

    A mutual aid collective dedicated to building community in San Jose, CA

  7. Hero Tent ↗ herotent.org

    San Jose abolitionist mutual aid nonprofit, founded in 2020. It supplies protesters, runs a Saturday free breakfast (Feed the Block) and does outreach with unhoused neighbors.

  8. San Jose Strong ↗ instagram.com

    Volunteer-ran org connecting South Bay folks to resources & creating in-house initiatives that build community
