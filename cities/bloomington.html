Bloomington

12 groups · Indiana

  1. Really Really Free Market ↗ instagram.com

    A grassroots organization fostering mutual aid and community support through free markets and resource sharing in Bloomington. They coordinate direct assistance, free item distribution, and solidarity networks while building power through community organizing and creating spaces for gift economy and collective care.

  2. McLean County Foster Closet ↗ mcfostercloset.org

    A grassroots organization fostering mutual aid and community power through support for foster families in McLean County. They coordinate resource distribution, direct assistance, and solidarity networks while building power through community care, family support, and creating pathways for foster children and families to thrive.

  3. Lunch for Peace ↗ lfpbloomington.org

    Project of the Center for Sustainable Living that runs outdoor Little Free Pantry boxes in Bloomington, Indiana, where anyone can leave or take food and hygiene items.

  4. Center for Sustainable Living ↗ simplycsl.org

    Bloomington nonprofit that runs community projects including a bike co-op, tool library, Little Free Pantry, Redbud Books, and solar programs.

  5. Pages to Prisoners ↗ pagestoprisoners.org

    A grassroots organization fostering mutual aid and community power through book distribution to incarcerated people. They coordinate book donations, letter writing, and solidarity networks while building power through educational access, resource sharing, and creating pathways for learning and connection across prison walls.

  6. Indiana Recovery Alliance ↗ indianarecoveryalliance.org

    Bloomington harm reduction group offering syringe access, naloxone, HIV/HCV testing, wound care, and street outreach for people who use drugs.

  7. Bloomington Anarchist Black Cross ↗ bloomingtonabc.noblogs.org

    An anarchist group focusing on solidarity with anarchist prisoners, prisoners of other liberatory struggles, and individuals engaged in prison rebellion.

  8. Bloomington ABC ↗ instagram.com

    Anarchist Black Cross chapter in Bloomington, Indiana. Sends monthly funds to anarchist prisoners, holds letter-writing nights, and runs the annual BAMF alternative media fest as a fundraiser.

  9. Bloomington DSA ↗ bloomingtondsa.org

    Bloomington, Indiana chapter of the Democratic Socialists of America. Runs housing, anti-surveillance and know-your-rights work, and hosts candidate forums.

  10. Exodus Refugee Immigration Bloomington ↗ instagram.com

    Bloomington office of Exodus Refugee Immigration, an Indiana refugee resettlement agency. Helps newly arrived refugees find housing and jobs and learn English.

  11. Bloomington WDS ↗ instagram.com
  12. Bloomington Not Bombs ↗ instagram.com
