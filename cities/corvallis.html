    Local United Way serving Linn, Benton and Lincoln counties, including Corvallis. It funds partner nonprofits and runs volunteer and local programs.

  3. Corvallis Mutual Aid Networkfacebook.com

    Grassroots community network coordinating direct support, resource sharing, and solidarity actions to meet immediate needs of Corvallis residents through mutual aid principles.

    Corvallis Palestine Solidarity is a community in Corvallis, Oregon, working to promote awareness and engage the public in the campaign for justice, equality and human rights for the Palestinian people.

    The Corvallis Daytime Drop-in Center (CDDC) is a community-based resource hub providing information, referrals, and direct services for people experiencing homelessness and poverty in Benton County and beyond.
