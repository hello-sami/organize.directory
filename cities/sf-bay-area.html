International Solidarity

  1. Bay Area for Palestine ↗ instagram.com

    Follow for action alerts, info, and safety tips. #PalestineWillBeFree

  2. Arab Resource & Organizing Center (AROC) ↗ araborganizing.org

    Provides legal, language-access, and case management services to low-income Arab and Muslim immigrants in the SF Bay Area, and organizes campaigns including for Palestine.

  3. Queers Undermining Israeli Terrorism (QUIT!) ↗ quitpalestine.org

    Bay Area queer group, founded in 2001, that organizes pickets, boycott (BDS) actions and protests against Israeli pinkwashing in support of Palestinian liberation.

  4. Bay Area PYM ↗ instagram.com

    Bay Area chapter of the @palestinianyouthmovement

  5. Bay Area United Against War (BAUAW) ↗ bauaw.org

    San Francisco-based anti-war newsletter and coalition covering dockworker actions against weapons shipments, drone warfare, and campaigns for political prisoners.

Indigenous Sovereignty

  1. Native American Health Center ↗ nativehealth.org

    Federally qualified community health center serving Native Americans, with medical, dental, behavioral health and WIC services at clinics in San Francisco and Oakland since 1972.

  2. Sogorea Te Land Trust ↗ sogoreate-landtrust.org

    Urban Indigenous women-led land trust in the East Bay that returns land to Indigenous people, runs the voluntary Shuumi Land Tax, and manages sacred sites.

  3. Idle No More SF Bay ↗ idlenomoresfbay.org

    Indigenous women-led volunteer group, founded in 2013, that organizes healing walks, marches and actions against the Bay Area's oil refineries, especially in the Richmond-to-Rodeo refinery corridor.

  4. Indigenous Anarchist Federation ↗ bsky.app

    Online platform sharing Indigenous anarchist ideas and struggles across the Americas, active on Bluesky.

Climate & Environmental Justice

  1. Asian Pacific Environmental Network (APEN) ↗ apen4ej.org

    Oakland-based environmental justice group organizing Asian immigrant and refugee communities in Oakland Chinatown, Richmond and Carson against polluters.

  2. Urban Habitat ↗ urbanhabitat.org

    Oakland nonprofit that trains low-income residents and residents of color for Bay Area boards and commissions, and does research and advocacy on transportation, housing, land use and climate policy.

  3. 1000 Grandmothers for Future Generations ↗ 1000grandmothers.com

    Group of elder women and allies in the Bay Area, based in Berkeley, doing nonviolent climate justice actions and supporting Indigenous and frontline communities.

  4. 350 Bay Area ↗ 350bayarea.org

    Regional grassroots climate group in the Bay Area, headquartered in Oakland, with local chapters in Marin, Sonoma, Napa, San Francisco, Contra Costa and the East Bay.

Community Support & Mutual Aid

  1. Homecooked Care for Liberation ↗ instagram.com

    Bay Area group that provides homemade meals to people organizing for Palestinian liberation.

  2. Common Humanity Collective ↗ commonhumanitycollective.org

    Bay Area mutual aid group that builds DIY air purifiers and distributes them free, along with masks and other supplies, through volunteer community builds.

  3. Bay Area Anarchist Coffeehouse ↗ instagram.com

    Bay Area group that hosts open-mic style potlucks bringing together anarchists and collectives from around the region.

  4. Bay Area Tenant and Neighborhood Councils ↗ baytanc.com

    Member-run tenant union with locals in Oakland, Berkeley, San Francisco and the South Bay, organizing renters and unhoused people against evictions, rent hikes and bad conditions.

  5. Bay Resistance ↗ bayresistance.org

    Network of Bay Area community groups, unions and neighborhood groups. Sends rapid response alerts on immigration raids and other threats, runs neighborhood pods, trainings and street campaigns.

Media & Educational Resources

  1. San Francisco Bay View National Black Newspaper ↗ sfbayview.com

    Independent Black newspaper founded in 1976, serving thought-provoking stories and commentary on the full range of Black trials and triumphs, with a focus on prison abolition, mutual aid, and community empowerment through grassroots journalism.

  2. Indybay - SF Bay Area Independent Media Center ↗ indybay.org

    Volunteer-run open-publishing news site where people post reports, photos, video and event listings about protests, labor, housing and other social movements in the Bay Area.

  3. Free Speech TV ↗ freespeech.org

    National nonprofit progressive news and opinion TV network covering social, economic and environmental justice. Based in Colorado; broadcasts nationally and streams online.

  4. CrimethInc. ↗ crimethinc.com

    Anarchist collective and publisher producing articles, books, zines, posters and podcasts on anti-authoritarian organizing. Operates as a decentralized network, not a local group.

  5. AK Press ↗ akpress.org

    Worker-run collective that publishes and distributes anarchist and radical-left books, based in Chico, California.

Economic & Worker Justice

  1. Mujeres Unidas y Activas (MUA) ↗ mujeresunidas.net

    Organization of Latina and Indigenous immigrant women in San Francisco, Oakland and Union City offering workshops, counseling and support, and campaigning for immigrant and domestic worker rights.

  2. Filipino Advocates for Justice ↗ filipinos4justice.org

    Filipino community organization in the East Bay, founded 1973. Runs youth programs, immigration services, worker rights support and civic engagement from offices in Oakland and Union City.

  3. Bay Area Workers Support (BAWS) ↗ bayareaworkerssupport.org

    Sex worker-led group in the Bay Area offering mutual aid grants, a bad date list, resource guides, and community events like an annual resource fair in Oakland.

  4. Alliance of South Asians Taking Action (ASATA) ↗ instagram.com

    The Alliance of South Asians Taking Action empowers South Asians to end violence, oppression, and exploitation within and against our communities.

Racial Justice

  1. SURJ Bay Area ↗ surjbayarea.org

    Bay Area chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice, organizing white people around racial justice. Current campaigns cover Oakland budget and community safety funding, and Palestine solidarity.

Anti-Repression & Prison Abolition

  1. Anti-Repression Bay Area ↗ instagram.com

    Providing protest support and bail funds for demonstrators through mutual aid networks.

  2. Community Works West ↗ communityworkswest.org

    Transforming justice through restorative practices, reentry support, and youth empowerment programs across the Bay Area, with a focus on healing and community-led solutions to incarceration.

  3. Flying Over Walls ↗ flyingoverwalls.org

    San Francisco-based queer and trans prisoner solidarity project that matches outside pen pals with incarcerated LGBTQ+ people and supports ongoing letter-writing.

  4. Prison Literature Project ↗ prisonlit.org

    By sending free reading materials to those behind bars, PLP aims to foster learning and critical thinking and help prepare people to lead successful lives after incarceration.

  5. Seeing Red Events ↗ seeingredevents.com

    A communist lecture and party series in the Bay Area.

  6. Lavender Phoenix ↗ lavenderphoenix.org

    Bay Area organization (formerly APIENC) of transgender, non-binary, and queer Asian and Pacific Islander people that trains grassroots leaders and runs member organizing programs.

  7. Bay Area Transformative Justice Collective ↗ batjc.org

    Oakland-based collective that runs trainings and support for transformative justice responses to harm, especially child sexual abuse.
