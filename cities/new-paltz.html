New Paltz

2 groups.
Part of New York.
  1. Millions of Butterfliesmillionsofbutterflies.org

    New Paltz, NY nonprofit founded in 2020 to address food insecurity. Started the village's Free Food Fridge in 2021 and runs Chrysalis Kitchen, back-to-school packages and bake sales.

  2. New Paltz Free Food Fridgeinstagram.com

    A community mutual aid initiative providing free food access at the Village Hall (25 Plattekill Ave). The fridge offers a space for neighbors to share food resources, operating on the principle that food is a human right and should be freely accessible to all community members.
