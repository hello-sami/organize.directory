Eugene

11 groups · Oregon

  1. SURJ Springfield-Eugene Oregon ↗ facebook.com

    Springfield-Eugene, Oregon chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice, which organizes white people around racial justice issues such as policing and immigrant driver licenses.

  2. ROAR Center ↗ instagram.com

    A community-led mutual aid center providing resources, organizing space, and support for local initiatives. ROAR (Resource Organizing and Radical) Center serves as a hub for community organizing, skill-sharing, and direct support in Eugene.

  3. Neighborhood Anarchists ↗ neighborhoodanarchists.org

    We organize locally in Eugene / Springfield Oregon to help build a society where neighbors support each other to meet basic needs, individuals are free to follow their passions, and empowered communities collectively shape the future.

  4. White Bird Clinic ↗ whitebirdclinic.org

    Eugene clinic serving low-income and unhoused people with medical, dental, addiction and crisis care, and runs the CAHOOTS mobile crisis team in Springfield.

  5. CORE Eugene ↗ coreeugene.org

    CORE empowers, and advocates for young people in Lane County.

  6. Food Not Bombs Eugene ↗ instagram.com

    Volunteers cook and serve free, mostly vegan meals in Eugene, Fridays at 4pm at the Park Blocks (8th Ave and Oak St) and some Sundays.

  7. Black Thistle Street Aid ↗ instagram.com

    Volunteer medical team giving free street outreach, monthly pop-up clinics, wound care and harm reduction supplies to unhoused people in Eugene-Springfield, OR.

  8. BareFoot Defenders ↗ instagram.com

    Advocacy and mutual aid group for unhoused people in Eugene and Springfield. Hands out homemade heaters and survival supplies, and protests camp sweeps.

  9. Eugene Tenant Alliance ↗ instagram.com

    Tenant advocacy group in Lane County. Lobbies the Eugene City Council and Oregon legislature for renter protections and endorses candidates through its PAC.

  10. Radical Alternative Development ↗ instagram.com

    Punk nonprofit in Eugene that runs all-ages benefit shows and offers mental health and suicide-prevention resources for youth and adults.

  11. Pro-Bone-O Veterinary Care ↗ instagram.com

    Nonprofit giving free vet exams, vaccines, food and supplies for pets of homeless people in Eugene, at clinics on the 2nd and 4th Sundays at the Eugene Mission.
