Minneapolis / St. Paul

37 groups · Minnesota

  1. Communities United Against Police Brutality (CUAPB) ↗ cuapb.org

    Minneapolis group that supports survivors and families of police violence, runs copwatch and a hotline, raises legal defense funds for activists, and pushes for police accountability.

  2. Families Supporting Families Against Police Violence ↗ facebook.com

    Support group founded in 2019 by Toshira Garraway for families who lost loved ones to police violence. Organizes marches and events and advocates for policy change in Minnesota.

  3. Minnesota Justice Coalition ↗ themjc.org

    Bloomington, MN nonprofit that helps families affected by police misconduct, runs an expungement clinic, drafts criminal justice legislation, and holds a holiday toy drive.

  4. Cordale Q Handy In Remembrance of Me Foundation ↗ cordaleqhandyinremembranceofme.org

    Foundation started by Kimberly Handy-Jones after St. Paul police killed her son Cordale. It pays for headstones and supports families who lost children to police and community violence.

  5. East Side Freedom Library ↗ eastsidefreedomlibrary.org

    Community library and event space at 1105 Greenbrier St in St. Paul's East Side, with programs on labor history, immigrant history, and social justice, plus walking tours and community events.

  6. May Day Books ↗ maydaybookstore.org

    Volunteer-run nonprofit leftist bookstore at 301 Cedar Ave in Minneapolis's West Bank, open since 1975. It hosts readings, film screenings, and organizing meetings.

  7. Birch Bark Books ↗ birchbarkbooks.com

    Independent Native-owned bookstore in Minneapolis's Kenwood neighborhood selling Indigenous literature, Native art, and books on tribal nations and language revitalization.

  8. Twin Cities Encampment Responders ↗ instagram.com

    We are individuals coming together to organize eviction defense, cop watch, & mutual aid for the Twin Cities encampments

  9. Black and Pink MPLS ↗ instagram.com

    Black & Pink MPLS works towards prison abolition in MN by providing pen pal matching to LGBTQIA2S+ folks on the inside.

  10. Black Garnet Books ↗ blackgarnetbooks.com

    Black-owned bookstore on University Ave in St. Paul's Midway area, stocking adult and YA fiction by Black and other authors of color, and hosting author and community events.

  11. Boneshaker Books ↗ boneshakerbooks.com

    Volunteer-run radical bookstore in the Wedge/LynLake area of Minneapolis, selling leftist books and hosting events. Moved to 708 W 26th St in late 2025.

  12. Blackbird Revolt ↗ blackbirdrevolt.com

    Black-owned abolitionist design and video studio in the Twin Cities that makes graphics for social justice groups and runs its own community projects and shop.

  13. Rainbow Collective for Change ↗ rainbowcollectiveforchange.org

    Twin Cities group for LGBTQIA+ families with kids ages 0-12. Runs playdates, meetups, a summer camp, free inclusive book giveaways, a book bus, and educator training.

  14. TIGERRS (Transgender, Intersex, Gender-Expansive Revolutionary Resources & Services) ↗ tigerrs.org

    TIGERRS is a nonhierarchical collective dedicated to delivering programs and resources that build solidarity and safety among transgender, intersex and gender-expansive Minnesotans.

  15. Minnesot Transgender Health Coalition ↗ mntransgenderhealth.org

    The Minnesota Transgender Health Coalition is committed to improving health care access and the quality of health care received by trans and gender non-conforming people through education, resources, and advocacy.

  16. Indigenous Peoples Task Force ↗ indigenouspeoplestf.org

    We were founded in 1987 to develop and implement culturally appropriate HIV education and direct services to the Native community in Minnesota.

  17. Women Against Military Madness (WAMM) ↗ womenagainstmilitarymadness.org

    Member-based Minneapolis group that opposes U.S. militarism and war. Runs campaigns on weapons makers, nuclear weapons and military spending, and holds an annual Walk Against Weapons.

  18. Southside Harm Reduction Services ↗ shrs.org

    South Minneapolis harm reduction group offering naloxone, syringe and safer-use supply delivery, street outreach, and a drop-in center on E Franklin Ave.

  19. Sex Workers Outreach Project (SWOP) Minneapolis ↗ instagram.com

    Volunteer-run, peer-led sex worker collective in Minneapolis–St. Paul doing outreach with safer-sex supplies and Narcan, plus decriminalization advocacy.

  20. Racial Justice Network (RJN) ↗ racialjusticenetwork.com

    Multi-racial Twin Cities group led by Nekima Levy-Armstrong, with committees on economic justice, criminal justice reform and education justice. Does outreach, direct action and press advocacy.

  21. Showing Up for Racial Justice (SURJ) - Twin Cities ↗ surjtc.org

    Twin Cities chapter of SURJ that organizes white people around racial and economic justice. Recent work includes ICE observation and boycotts of Target and Home Depot.

  22. Library Patrons Union ↗ librarypatronsunion.com

    Community-run union of Hennepin County Library patrons that organizes for more just public libraries, including protests over library policing and trespass policies.

  23. St. Paul Mutual Aid ↗ facebook.com

    Public Facebook group where Midway neighbors in St. Paul coordinate non-police, non-governmental mutual aid and care. Started after George Floyd's murder in 2020.

  24. Sanctuary Supply Depot ↗ instagram.com

    We are a central hub aimed at facilitating the collection and distribution of basic survival supplies for our unhoused neighbors.

  25. Gertrude Brown Community Land Trust ↗ gbclt.org

    Minneapolis community land trust run by formerly and currently unhoused people, working to buy land for cooperatively run micro-homes for unhoused neighbors.

  26. Community Kitchen MPLS ↗ instagram.com

    Minneapolis mutual aid group that cooks and delivers several hundred hot meals a week to unhoused neighbors. Volunteers cook on Sundays and deliver Mondays and Tuesdays.

  27. SPIRAL Collective ↗ instagram.com

    Raising money for Lyft rides for abortions and virtual trainings for abortion doulas

  28. People's Anti-Imperialist Study & Action ↗ instagram.com

    Third-World diasporas in 'mpls/stp' building support for revolutionary struggles/liberation movements in & beyond amerika. Hosts film screenings, teach-ins, and art builds against imperialism.

  29. Camp Nenookaasi ↗ instagram.com

    A community based healing camp rooted in native practices & inclusive of all unsheltered relatives

  30. North Star Health Collective ↗ northstarhealthcollective.org

  31. Twin Cities Logistics Collective ↗ twincitieslogistics.org

  32. Southside Kwes ↗ instagram.com

  33. Autonomous Yurt Union - Minneapolis ↗ instagram.com

  34. Grapevine Collective ↗ grapevinecollective.org

  35. Autonomous Yurt Union St. Paul ↗ instagram.com

  36. North Country Food Alliance ↗ ncfa-mn.org

  37. Home Base Central ↗ facebook.com

    A collective dedicated to housing justice, support for the unhoused, and mutual aid.
