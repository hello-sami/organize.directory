Washington DC

14 groups.
Part of District of Columbia.

    Volunteer group in DC's Ward 2 that gives monthly groceries to about 150 low-income immigrant households and weekly supplies to unhoused neighbors.

  3. SURJ DCinstagram.com

    DC chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice. Organizes white people to support Black- and POC-led groups in DC, with deep canvassing and migrant justice teams.

  4. Harriet's Wildest Dreamsharrietsdreams.org

    Black-led community defense hub in Southeast DC with organizing, rapid-response, and legal defense programs. Works with DC Mutual Aid Network and Stop Police Terror.

  5. Stop Police Terror DCsptdc.com

    Works against racist, militarized policing in Washington DC. Campaigns to defund MPD, end stop-and-frisk and fully implement the NEAR Act.

  6. Family & Friends of Incarcerated People (FFOIP)ffoip.org

    DC group that runs youth development programs, retreats and outings for children of incarcerated parents and at-risk youth in the DC area.

  7. Beloved Community Incubatorbelovedcommunityincubator.org

    DC-area nonprofit that helps start and convert worker-owned co-ops, runs the DC SELF solidarity loan fund, and a guaranteed income project.

  8. Food Not Bombs DCinstagram.com

    Volunteer group that has served free vegan meals to people living outside in downtown DC every Saturday since 2013, made from donated and surplus food. Meals are at McPherson Square.

  9. Claudia Jones Schoolclaudiajonesschool.org

  10. East of the River Mutual Aidinstagram.com

    Part of DC Mutual Network. Representing ward 7 and 8

  11. Free Minds Book Clubfreemindsbookclub.org

    DC nonprofit running book clubs and writing workshops for people in the DC Jail and federal prisons, plus a reentry book club and job readiness program for returning members.

  12. DC IWOC (Incarcerated Workers Organizing Committeeinstagram.com

    Resisting the state through an abolitionist inside-outside network, on occupied Piscataway, Manahoac, Appamattuck, Chesapeake & Tutelo lands

  13. Baldwin House Community Collectivebaldwin-house.org

    Community-led affordable housing cooperative and mutual aid hub creating sustainable, community-controlled housing and organizing spaces for working class Washingtonians.

  14. The Palm Collectiveinstagram.com

    Black, Queer-led organization building a coalition of grassroots social justice entrepreneurial organizations, centering climate & environmental justice.
