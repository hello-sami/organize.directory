Santa Cruz

8 groups · California

  1. Really Really Free Market - Santa Cruz ↗ instagram.com

    Monthly free market in Santa Cruz held the second Sunday at SubRosa, 703 Pacific Ave. Nothing is bought, sold or traded; people bring and take items.

  2. SubRosa ↗ subrosaproject.org

    Collectively run anarchist community space at 703 Pacific Ave, Santa Cruz. Hosts events, classes, meetings and an anarchist lending library.

  3. Santa Cruz Hub for Sustainable Living ↗ santacruzhub.org

    A constellation of mutual aid projects supporting skill-sharing and sustainable communities since 1994. The Hub hosts multiple initiatives including The Bike Church (community bike shop), The Fábrica (textile arts workspace), Hard Core Compost, PedX Courier & Cargo, and Tenant Sanctuary, while fostering interconnected networks of community care and ecological stewardship.

  4. Tenant Sanctuary ↗ tenantsanctuary.org

    A tenant rights education project providing direct support and mutual aid through rights counseling, education, and community organizing. They offer in-person, phone, and email counseling on issues like rent increases, evictions, deposits, and habitability while building tenant power through collective knowledge and solidarity.

  5. Homeless United for Friendship and Freedom (HUFF) ↗ huffsantacruz.org

    Santa Cruz homeless rights group. Meets Thursdays at SubRosa, documents police actions against encampments, and runs a radio show on Free Radio Santa Cruz.

  6. Santa Cruz Welcoming Network ↗ santacruzwelcomingnetwork.com

    Volunteer-run group in Santa Cruz County, active since 2019, that helps asylum seekers and refugees resettle. Has over 100 volunteers.

  7. SURJ Santa Cruz County ↗ surjsantacruzcounty.org

    Santa Cruz County chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice. Organizes white people for racial justice through events, book circles, and donations to local BIPOC-led groups.

  8. Santa Cruz Food Not Bombs ↗ santacruz.foodnotbombs.net

    Shares free vegan meals and survival gear in downtown Santa Cruz on weekends, noon to 3pm, and campaigns for shelter for unhoused people.
