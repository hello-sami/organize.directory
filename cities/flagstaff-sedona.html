Flagstaff-Sedona

7 groups.
Part of Arizona.
  1. Flagstaff Community Coalitioninstagram.com

    A grassroots collective fostering mutual aid and community solidarity in Flagstaff. They coordinate direct assistance, resource sharing, and community care initiatives while building power through collective action and creating spaces for mutual support and community resilience.

  2. Flagstaff Community Fridgesinstagram.com

    Runs a community fridge in Flagstaff, AZ where people leave food they can spare and take what they need, stocked partly with local produce and open 24/7.

  3. Native Americans for Community Action (NACA)nacainc.org

    Nonprofit in Flagstaff providing primary care, behavioral health, wellness programs, and youth and social support services, mainly for Native people.

  4. Flagstaff Prideflagstaffpride.org

    Flagstaff nonprofit that supports the 2SLGBTQIA+ community in Northern Arizona and puts on the annual Pride in the Pines festival, parade and Pride weekend events.

  5. Flagstaff Queer Collectiveinstagram.com

    Social group for LGBTQIA2S+ people and allies in Flagstaff that hosts meetups such as craft nights, coffee hangouts, cleanups, and Pride events.

  6. Flagstaff Students for Justice in Palestineinstagram.com

    Student group in Flagstaff organizing marches, teach-ins and campus actions in support of Palestine; it staged the April 2024 NAU encampment.

  7. Flock out of Flaginstagram.com

    Flagstaff campaign that pushed the city to end its contract with Flock Safety license plate cameras over privacy concerns. The council canceled the contract in December 2025.
