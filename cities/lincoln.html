Lincoln, Nebraska

7 groups · Nebraska

  1. Star City Pride Events ↗ starcityprideevents.org

    Lincoln, Nebraska nonprofit that produces LGBTQIA2S+ events, including the Pride Festival and Parade, Lavender Square Market, Pride Gala and a book club.

  2. Common Root ↗ commonroot.org

    A volunteer-run community center at 211 N 14th St in Lincoln that hosts free events: movie screenings, Free Saturday giveaways, art shows, skill-shares and festivals. It also makes activist buttons.

  3. The Black Cat House ↗ theblackcathouse.org

    Collective-run community space at 1601 B St, Lincoln, NE that holds weekly general meetings and events, keeps a library and meeting archive, and hosts outside groups.

  4. Lincoln DSA ↗ dsanebraska.org

    Lincoln chapter of the Democratic Socialists of America. Hosts brake light clinics, socials, book clubs and working group meetings.

  5. Everett Free Grocery Program ↗ facebook.com

    Free food and hygiene item distribution program in the Everett neighborhood of Lincoln, Nebraska.

  6. /r/LincolnMutualAid ↗ reddit.com

    A hub for mutual aid resources in the Lincoln, Nebraska community. Taking care of our neighbors in a time of rising fascism and oligarchy.

  7. Lincoln Community Care ↗ instagram.com

    Weekly distribution in solidarity with our unhoused neighbors in LNK
