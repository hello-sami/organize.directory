Regional Organizations9

  1. East Bay Food Not Bombseastbayfoodnotbombs.org

    We are an all-volunteer collective that serves vegan food in Oakland and Berkeley six days a week. In addition to the seven hot meals we serve each week, we distribute bagged lunches and dinners out of our truck and bicycle across the East Bay.

  2. Center for Empowering Refugees and Immigrants (CERI)cerieastbay.org

    Community organization providing culturally attuned mental health services, LGBTQ+ advocacy, youth programs, and community care for refugee and immigrant communities in Oakland, with a focus on Southeast Asian genocide survivors and their families.

  3. Centro Legal de la Razacentrolegal.org

    Community legal organization providing direct legal services, community education, and advocacy for immigrant, tenant, and worker rights in Oakland, with a focus on serving low-income and immigrant communities through mutual aid and solidarity.

  4. Village of Lovethevillageoflove.org

    Nonprofit that runs the Alameda Day Center, overnight shelter, safe parking and street outreach for unhoused people in Alameda, Oakland and Berkeley.

  5. Justice Reinvestment Coalition of Alameda Countyurbanstrategies.org

    Coalition of community groups in Alameda County pushing to cut incarceration and shift county funding toward housing, mental health care, and other services.

  6. UC Gill Tract Community Farmgilltractfarm.org

    Community farm at San Pablo and Marin Aves in Albany, CA, on UC land. Grows food and herbs, runs a pay-what-you-can farm stand, and offers agroecology and herbal classes.

  7. East Bay Alternative Book and Zine Festeastbayalternativebookandzinefest.com

    Annual volunteer-run, nonprofit zine and alternative book fair in Berkeley where 100+ East Bay and Bay Area zinesters and small publishers sell and share their work.

  8. Albany and El Cerrito for Palestineinstagram.com

    Grassroots group of Albany and El Cerrito, CA residents organizing for Palestine: protests, human billboards, film screenings, and calls for divestment from Israel and an arms embargo.

  9. East Bay Housing Organizations (EBHO)ebho.org

    EBHO is a membership-based advocacy organization fighting for affordable housing for all.
