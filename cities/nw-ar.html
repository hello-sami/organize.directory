Community Support

  1. Northwest Arkansas Democratic Socialists of America ↗ dsanwa.org

    Northwest Arkansas chapter of Democratic Socialists of America, with working groups on Gaza solidarity, electoral politics, labor, public transit and mutual aid.

  2. NWA Socialist Alternative ↗ instagram.com

    Northwest Arkansas branch of Socialist Alternative, a national socialist organization.

  3. Hands Up NWA ↗ facebook.com

    A grassroots organization fostering mutual aid and community support through direct action and solidarity initiatives in Northwest Arkansas. They coordinate resource sharing, community organizing, and support networks while building power through local advocacy and creating spaces for collective care.

  4. Mayday NWA ↗ instagram.com

    A community-based mutual aid network in Northwest Arkansas focused on direct support and solidarity initiatives. They work to build community power through resource sharing, emergency assistance, and collective organizing while fostering sustainable networks of care and support.

Food Justice

  1. NWA Food Not Bombs ↗ instagram.com

    Volunteer group that recovers food and serves free vegetarian and vegan meals in Walker Park, Fayetteville, on the last Sunday of each month at noon.

International Solidarity

  1. NWA for Palestine ↗ instagram.com

    Northwest Arkansas group organizing rallies and marches in Fayetteville in support of Palestinians, including weekly protests in 2024.

  2. Friends of Palestine NWA ↗ instagram.com

    Palestine solidarity group in Northwest Arkansas that holds weekly demonstrations in Fayetteville and educates the community about Palestine. Has affiliated groups in Little Rock and Fort Smith.

LGBTQ+ Support

  1. The Equality Crew ↗ theequalitycrew.org

    Springdale-based nonprofit running social programs for LGBTQ+ youth in Northwest Arkansas, including D&D campaigns, an inclusive prom, Rainbow Library book pop-ups and a teen council.
