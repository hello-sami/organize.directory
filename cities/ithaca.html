Local Listings Ithaca, New York

Ithaca

4 groups.
Part of New York.
  1. Durland Alternatives Libraryalternativeslibrary.org

    Nonprofit library at Cornell University in Ithaca, founded in 1974, with books and zines on social change. Also runs Prisoner Express, which corresponds with incarcerated people.

  2. Tompkins County Workers' Centertcworkerscenter.org

    Labor advocacy group in Ithaca, NY. Runs a workers' rights helpline, a living wage certification program, and worker organizing campaigns.

  3. No Mas Lagrimasnomaslagrimasithaca.org

    A non-profit organization providing mutual aid and direct support to individuals and families in Tompkins County. They coordinate food rescue and distribution, community celebrations, youth programs, and support for domestic violence victims and recently incarcerated individuals. Regular food distribution takes place Thursdays 4-6pm at the Henry Saint John Building, with additional food box deliveries throughout Tompkins County.

  4. Flock Off! Ithacainstagram.com

    Ithaca residents campaigning against Flock Safety license plate cameras. They pushed the city to end its Flock contract in 2026 and have pressed for the cameras' removal.
