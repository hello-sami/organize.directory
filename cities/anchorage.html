Anchorage

3 groups.
Part of Alaska.
  1. Mutual Aid Network of Anchorage (MANA)wearemana.org

    Volunteer network in Anchorage that pairs neighbors who need help with volunteers for grocery deliveries, wellness checks, yard work and navigating social services. Fiscally sponsored by AKPIRG.

  2. Food for Thought Alaskafoodforthoughtalaska.com

    Anchorage food activism group founded in 2018 that runs a network of free community fridges in neighborhoods including Fairview, Muldoon, Mountain View, Spenard and Government Hill.

  3. Choosing Our Rootschoosingourroots.org

    Queer-led and queer-focused nonprofit providing housing support and case management to queer, Alaskan youth and young adults experiencing housing insecurity and homelessness.
