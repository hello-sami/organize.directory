Housing Justice5

  1. San Francisco Tenants Unionsftu.org

    Member-supported organization fighting for tenants' rights and housing justice through direct support, education, and collective organizing.

  2. Coalition on Homelessnesscohsf.org

    San Francisco group, founded 1987, that organizes unhoused people and service providers for housing and against sweeps and citations; publishes the Street Sheet newspaper.

  3. Eviction Defense Collaborativeevictiondefense.org

    San Francisco nonprofit providing free legal representation and rental assistance to tenants facing eviction. Runs the city's Tenant Right to Counsel program from 976 Mission St.

  4. SF Anti-Displacement Coalitionsfadc.org

    Coalition of San Francisco tenant organizations and allies organizing against evictions and rent increases. Holds tenant assemblies and free legal clinics.

  5. San Francisco Food Not Bombsinstagram.com

    Volunteers serve free vegetarian meals in San Francisco on Wednesdays at 16th & Mission and Fridays at 9th St.

Community Organizations7

  1. PODER SFpodersf.org

    Organizes Latinx immigrant families and youth in San Francisco's Mission, Excelsior and southeast neighborhoods on environmental and economic justice issues, climate resilience and civic engagement.

  2. Chinese Progressive Associationcpasf.org

    Grassroots organization building collective power with working-class Chinese immigrants through mutual aid, worker and tenant organizing, youth leadership development, and environmental justice initiatives.

  3. People Power Mediapeoplepowermedia.org

    San Francisco housing justice group that makes popular education media (like the animated short Priced Out), runs workshops, and staffs the REP-SF planning coalition.

  4. SF Mutual Aidsfmutualaid.com

    A community network coordinating mutual aid efforts and connecting neighbors to share resources across San Francisco through direct support, solidarity, and collective care.

  5. GLIDEglide.org

    Tenderloin social services organization at 330 Ellis St in San Francisco, offering meals, health and recovery services, housing support, and advocacy for people in poverty.

  6. The Women's Buildingwomensbuilding.org

    Mission District community center, in its building since 1979, offering a food pantry, legal aid, financial coaching, tax clinics, and job search help for women, immigrants, and LGBTQ+ people.

  7. Resistance SFinstagram.com

    General protest calendar and community organizing resource.

LGBTQ+ Resources5

  1. Queerious Labsqueeriouslabs.com

    Queer art and technology workshop and hackerspace in SoMa, San Francisco, hosting classes, events, and projects like digitizing the On Our Backs archive.

  2. The Sisters of Perpetual Indulgencethesisters.org

    San Francisco order of queer and trans nuns, active since 1979, doing community service, fundraising grants, and public events, plus a campaign on intimate partner violence.

  3. Lavender Phoenixlavenderphoenix.org

    Bay Area organization (formerly APIENC) of transgender, non-binary, and queer Asian and Pacific Islander people that trains grassroots leaders and runs member organizing programs.

  4. SF LGBT Centersfcenter.org

    Community center at 1800 Market St. in San Francisco offering LGBTQ+ employment, financial, and youth services, plus community programs and events.

  5. Gay Shameinstagram.com

    Queer and trans activist group in San Francisco, founded in 2001, that protests corporate Pride, gentrification and policing through street actions and theater.

Arts & Culture2

  1. Silver Sprocketsilversprocket.net

    Independent comics publisher and art collective in San Francisco that publishes zines, comics and graphic novels. Its Valencia St. shop closed in March 2026; publishing and online sales continue.

  2. Betti Onobettiono.com

    Oakland nonprofit led by Black women and survivors, founded 2010; runs artist residencies, youth fellowships, a mobile cultural hub and programs to end gender-based violence.
