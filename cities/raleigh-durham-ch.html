Triangle Area

14 groups · North Carolina

  1. Feed Durham NC ↗ instagram.com

    A grassroots mutual aid collective organizing food distribution and community support in Durham. They coordinate food rescue, resource sharing, and direct support while building sustainable networks of food access and community care.

  2. Durham Beyond Policing ↗ durhambeyondpolicing.org

    Abolitionist group in Durham, NC that pushes to move city money from policing into non-police crisis response like the HEART program, and runs popular education on transformative justice.

  3. Pauli Murray Center for History & Social Justice ↗ paulimurraycenter.com

    A historic site and community center dedicated to advancing social justice through preserving the legacy of Rev. Dr. Pauli Murray, a groundbreaking civil rights activist, legal scholar, feminist, author, and Episcopal priest. They coordinate educational programs, community dialogues, arts initiatives, and mutual aid organizing while connecting Durham's history of activism to contemporary human rights issues.

  4. Rainbow Collective for Change ↗ rainbowcollectiveforchange.org

    Runs play dates, story times, summer camps, and classroom book kits for LGBTQIA+ families with children ages 0-12, with chapters in Durham, NC and the Twin Cities, MN.

    Group of artists in the North Carolina Triangle who raise mutual aid funds through art sales and events, including community potlucks.

  6. Durham Prison Letterwriting ↗ instagram.com

  7. Comrades in the Commons ↗ instagram.com

  10. Carrboro Free Market ↗ instagram.com
