Social Justice Organizations

  1. Unraveled Press ↗ unraveledpress.com

    Chicago-based independent news outlet doing investigative and on-the-ground reporting, owned by its journalists and funded entirely by reader donations.

  2. Kenwood Oakland Community Organization ↗ kocoonline.org

    By engaging low-income and working families, KOCO develops leaders who impact public policy and work hard to improve life in local communities.

  3. Little Village Environmental Justice Organization (LVEJO) ↗ lvejo.org

    Community group in Little Village, Chicago, organizing for clean air, safer water, and green space, with food justice and youth organizing programs.

  4. The TRiiBE ↗ thetriibe.com

    Independent, Black-owned digital news outlet in Chicago publishing journalism, documentary, and culture coverage about Black Chicago.

  5. PO Box Collective ↗ poboxcollective.us

    An art and mutual aid space in a former post office in Rogers Park that hosts free workshops, exhibitions and community events and runs a free store.

  6. Chicago Alliance Against Racist and Political Repression (CAARPR) ↗ caarpr.org

    Chicago group, founded 1973, that organizes on police accountability, support for people in prison, and immigrant rights, and defends Black and other oppressed communities against repression.

  7. #LetUsBreathe Collective ↗ letusbreathecollective.com

    Chicago alliance of artists and activists working toward a world without police and prisons. Runs the Breathing Room, a South Side space for arts, organizing and healing.

  8. Southside Together ↗ southsidetogether.org

    Organizes Black, poor and working-class residents in Woodlawn, South Shore and nearby South Side neighborhoods on tenant rights, housing and a community benefits agreement. Formed by a 2024 merger of STOP and Not Me We.

  9. Dare to Struggle - Chicago ↗ instagram.com

    Chicago chapter of Dare to Struggle, an activist group organizing on police brutality, mass incarceration, immigration enforcement and housing.

  10. Southwest Collective ↗ swcollective.org

    A neighborhood group on Chicago's Southwest Side that runs food distributions, free baby-gear giveaways, tree planting and street-safety campaigns across eight neighborhoods.

Healthcare

  1. Brave Space Alliance ↗ bravespacealliance.org

    Black-led, trans-led LGBTQ+ community center in Hyde Park on Chicago's South Side. Offers a clothing closet, food pantry, support groups, housing help, health services and a harm reduction station.

  2. Support Ho(s)e ↗ sxhxcollective.org

    A collective of sex workers and allies providing mutual aid, harm reduction, and community support.

  3. Chicago Abortion Fund ↗ chicagoabortionfund.org

    A mutual aid organization providing financial, logistical, and emotional support to people seeking abortion care in Chicago and Illinois. They work to make abortion accessible to everyone through direct funding assistance and community support networks.

  4. AIDS Foundation Chicago ↗ aidschicago.org

    Chicago nonprofit, founded in 1985, that funds and coordinates HIV prevention, care, and housing services and advocates on HIV and housing policy.

  5. Chicago Action Medical ↗ chicagoactionmedical.org

    Chicago street medic collective, active since 2002. It gives first aid at protests and runs street medic and first aid trainings.

  6. Chi Care ↗ instagram.com

    Help us provide warm meals, clean water, basic necessities, and a restored sense of dignity to individuals experiencing homelessness in Chicagoland.

  7. Sex Workers Outreach Project Chicago ↗ swopchicago.org

    Sex Workers Outreach Project Chicago is a Black-led, grassroots organization dedicated to providing direct aid and advocacy for anyone working in the sex industry.

Prison Abolition and Prisoner Solidarity

  1. Believers Bail Out ↗ believersbailout.org

    We are a community led bail fund freeing Muslims from pre-trial & immigration incarceration.

  2. Chicago Community Jail Support ↗ chicago-community-jail-support.webnode.page

    Volunteer-run daily mutual aid outside Cook County Jail in Chicago, giving people on release phone calls, clothing, food, rides home, and emergency housing.

  3. Pushing Envelopes Chicago ↗ instagram.com

    Volunteer-run Chicago group, formerly Black and Pink Chicago, that matches LGBTQ+ people in Illinois prisons with pen pals, sends a free newsletter, and supports people after release.

  4. Midwest Books to Prisoners ↗ midwestbookstoprisoners.org

    All-volunteer group in Chicago that mails free books to incarcerated people who write in with requests, since 2004. Volunteer hours are held at Theatre Y in North Lawndale.

  5. Chicago Torture Justice Center ↗ chicagotorturejustice.org

    A Center for survivors of police torture in Chicago. We are part of & support the movement to end all forms of police violence

  6. Defund CPD Campaign ↗ instagram.com

    Chicago campaign, started in 2020 by the Black Abolitionist Network, to cut the police department budget by 75% and put the money into community programs.

Migrant Support

  1. Refugee Community Connection ↗ refugeecommunityconnection.wordpress.com

    RCC is an all-volunteer Chicago based mutual aid organization that seeks to ease the transition of our newly arrived neighbors (refugees, asylees, and others forced to flee their home country) into permanent housing in the Chicago area

  2. Nuevos Vecinos Free Store ↗ instagram.com

    Shopping at the Nuevos Vecinos Tienda Gratis (New Neighbors Free Store) is often the first opportunity for new arrivals to replace the clothes and shoes that have served them for more than 4,000 miles.

  3. Casa Al-Fatiha ↗ linktr.ee

    Casa Al-Fatiha is an autonomous, Chicago-based, sanctuary house for LGBTQ+ asylum seekers, people released from detention, and peer supporters.
