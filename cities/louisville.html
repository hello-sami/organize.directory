Louisville

4 groups · Kentucky

  1. Louisville Showing Up for Racial Justice (LSURJ) ↗ louisvillesurj.org

    Louisville chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice. Organizes white people in Louisville around campaigns like Care Not Cages and a Community Defense Network, plus canvassing and trainings.

  2. Change Today, Change Tomorrow ↗ change-today.org

    Black woman-led nonprofit founded in 2019 in Louisville's West End. Runs food distribution (Feed the West, Zero Hunger Mobile Market), youth programs and public health work.

  3. Collective Liberation Network ↗ instagram.com

    Louisville group that coordinated mutual aid for queer and trans BIPOC leaders during the 2020 uprising, including meals for protesters and amplifying organizing opportunities.

  4. Louisville Workers Brigade ↗ instagram.com

    Louisville workers' publication and group that writes about local union drives and strikes and hosts panels with workers organizing their workplaces.
