Sacramento

13 groups.
Part of Northern California, California.
  1. The Reclamation Projecttrpcoalition.org

    Youth-run grassroots organization building mutual aid initiatives, political education, and community healing programs for marginalized youth, with a focus on BIPOC, immigrant/refugee, and LGBTQ+ communities.

  2. Lavender Librarylavenderlibrary.com

    Community-run LGBTQ+ library and archive providing free access to queer literature, history, and cultural resources through mutual aid and solidarity.

  3. Cellblock 2 Cityblockinstagram.com

    Re-entry support organization helping formerly incarcerated people transition back to the community through mutual aid and solidarity.

  4. Camp Resolutioninstagram.com

    A self-governing homeless encampment in Sacramento that provides a safe, community-run space for unhoused individuals with support from local nonprofits and the city.

  5. Sacramento Homeless Unionsacramentohomelessunion.org

    Sacramento chapter of the National Union of the Homeless. It gives supplies and clothing to unhoused people and brings legal challenges against the city over sweeps, shelter, and heat protections.

  6. Sac Social Justice Eventsinstagram.com

    Local activist posting event announcements.

  7. Sacramento Punks with Lunchsacpwl.wordpress.com

    Volunteer mutual aid collective that runs weekend distributions of meals, clothes, survival supplies and harm reduction supplies for housed and unhoused neighbors in Sacramento.

  8. Anti Police-Terror Project (APTP) Sacramentoantipoliceterrorproject.org

    Sacramento chapter of the Anti Police-Terror Project. Runs MH First mental health crisis response, twice-monthly jail support with Decarcerate Sacramento, and care packages for families affected by police violence.

  9. Sacramento Area Peace Actionsacpeace.org

    All-volunteer Sacramento chapter of national Peace Action. Holds weekly peace vigils and campaigns against war, nuclear weapons and US military spending.

  10. SURJ Sacramentosurjsacramento.org

    Sacramento chapter of Showing Up for Racial Justice. Runs racial justice study groups (Learn & Liberate) and community meetings, and raises money and turnout for partners like NorCal Resist and CHIRP.

  11. Sacramento Food Not Bombsinstagram.com

    Serving a free vegan meal the 2nd and 4th Sunday of the month at 1:30pm on the corner of 10th & J. If you're interested in joining, message us!

  12. Sacramento Valley IWWsaciww.com

    Sacramento Valley branch of the Industrial Workers of the World. Offers free organizer trainings, has committees for food/retail and public service workers, and supports the Sacramento IWOC.

  13. Black Lives Matter Sacramentoinstagram.com

    Racial justice organization that also hosts a community garden
