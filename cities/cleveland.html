Cleveland

12 groups.
Part of Ohio.
  1. Black Lives Matter Clevelandblmcle216.org

    Cleveland chapter formed in December 2015 after the killing of Tamir Rice. Runs a mutual aid fund for families affected by police and gun violence and organizes direct actions.

  2. LGBT Community Center of Greater Clevelandlgbtcleveland.org

    LGBTQ+ community center at 6600 Detroit Ave in Cleveland running support groups, youth and 50+ programs, events, and the annual Pride in the CLE.

  3. The Rhizome Houserhizomehouse.org

    Radical social center and infoshop at 2174 Lee Road, Cleveland Heights. Hosts reading groups, workshops and community cleanups, and runs a lending library. Open Sundays 1-4pm.

  4. Burning River Anarchist Collectivefacebook.com

    Cleveland anarchist collective founded in 2000. Runs a not-for-profit book distro and publisher and a lending library of over 2,600 titles.

  5. Cleveland Pandemic Responsecprcle.org

    A group of neighborhood organizers and community members aiming to provide mutual aid and support to NEO residents during the COVID-19 pandemic.

  6. Food Not Bombs Lake County and East Side CLEinstagram.com

    We share free fresh fruits and vegetables twice a month in Willoughby, and twice a month on Cleveland’s east side.

  7. Westside Cleveland Food Not Bombsinstagram.com

    FNB is a national movement that opposes militarism and provides free food for the public, no questions asked.

  8. The InterReligious Task Force on Central America (IRTF)instagram.com

    IRTF is a Cleveland-based interfaith group that promotes solidarity, human rights, and collective liberation.

  9. Cleveland Liberation Centercleliberationcenter.carrd.co

    Socialist community space at 9801 Denison Ave in Cleveland, run by PSL Cleveland, hosting forums, film screenings, open mics, and organizing meetings.

  10. The People's Distrothepeoplesdistro.carrd.co

    Worker-run group in Cleveland that gives out free fresh produce, clothes, and other goods in neighborhoods, with no one turned away.

  11. Party for Socialism and Liberation - Clevelandinstagram.com

    Cleveland branch of the Party for Socialism and Liberation, a socialist party. Holds public forums and protests and runs political education in Cleveland.

  12. JVP Clevelandinstagram.com

    Cleveland chapter of Jewish Voice for Peace, organizing Jews in solidarity with Palestinian freedom through protests and advocacy.
