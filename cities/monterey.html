White Pages Monterey, California

Monterey

4 groups.
Part of California's Central Coast, California.
  1. Whites for Racial Equityfacebook.com

    Monterey County group of white people working on racial justice. Hosts monthly book and movie discussions and trainings, and is a local affiliate of Showing Up for Racial Justice.

  2. Old Capitol Booksoldcapitolbooks.com

    Used bookstore at 482 Alvarado St in downtown Monterey. Hosts an anarchist discussion group, Marx's Capital reading groups, poetry workshops and philosophy discussions.

  3. Peace Resource Centerpeacecentral.wordpress.com

    A peace and social justice center in Seaside, CA, with a free lending library, film screenings, talks, workshops and ESL classes. It also hosts the Monterey County weekly peace calendar.

  4. Ayudantes De La Tierrasupportfarmworkers.org

    Delivers resources to farmworker families in the Salinas Valley and does advocacy and education for them. Started in summer 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic and the wildfires.
