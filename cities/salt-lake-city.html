White Pages Salt Lake City, Utah

Salt Lake City

7 groups.
Part of Utah.
  1. Under the Umbrellaundertheumbrellabookstore.com

    A queer-owned and operated bookstore providing a safe community space and mutual aid resources for the LGBTQ+ community in Salt Lake City. They offer a carefully curated selection of books centering queer voices and experiences, while fostering community connections and support through events, book clubs, and accessible pricing options.

  2. Salt Lake Community Mutual Aidslcmutualaid.com

    We are a mutual aid collective practicing harm reduction and working to build resilient community structures in the Salt Lake Valley. Runs community fridges.

  3. Food Not Bombs Salt Lake Cityinstagram.com

    Tues, Thurs and Sat. 11am at Richmond Park 444e 600s SLC, UT since 2004. Come get free food!

  4. Salt Lake Harm Reduction Projectshrpexchange.org

    Syringe Exchange Program in SLC offering safe disposal, education, and COVID resources at no cost.

  5. Salt Lake Community Bail Fundslcbailfund.org

    Posts bail for people held in Salt Lake County jail and runs court support for people facing criminal and immigration hearings. Works to end pretrial detention in Utah.

  6. Wasatch Tenants Unitedwasatchtenantsunited.org

    Volunteer-run tenants' group in Salt Lake City that organizes rallies, City Council pack-ins, call-ins, neighborhood BBQs and mutual aid for renters.

  7. Armed Queers Salt Lake Cityinstagram.com

    The Armed Queers are a revolutionary LGBTQ organization dedicated to the defense, and success, of oppressed people’s movements.
