Kansas City

11 groups · Missouri

  1. Turnsol Books ↗ instagram.com

    Worker-owned bookstore, coffee shop and event space in Kansas City's Crossroads district, selling radical, queer and independent-press books and zines and hosting writing programs.

  2. Neither/nor Zine Distro ↗ facebook.com

    A Kansas City zine distro and library, running since 2015. It sells zines at local shops, offers a zine subscription and hosts a monthly zine club and workshops.

  3. KC Mutual Aid ↗ kcmutualaid.com

    Kansas City group started in 2020 that runs a community garden, fridge and pantry, Free Skool workshops and a twice-yearly Mutual Aid Day conference.

  4. Kansas City Mutual Aid Network ↗ facebook.com

    Kansas City-area mutual aid group started in 2020. Runs a community garden, fridge and pantry, Free Skool workshops, and a twice-yearly Mutual Aid Day.

  5. Food Not Bombs KC ↗ fnbkc.org

    A volunteer-run mutual aid collective sharing free vegan meals and community support in Kansas City for over a decade. They coordinate weekly food distribution every Sunday at Independence & Monroe Avenue, recover food that would otherwise be discarded, and provide supplies to support strikes, marches, and demonstrations. Operating under the principle of "solidarity, not charity," they work to address food insecurity while protesting war, poverty, and environmental destruction.

  6. Trans Joy KC ↗ instagram.com

    Kansas City group that hosts Trans Joy in the Park meetups and Trans Joy Camp, a pay-what-you-can camping retreat for trans and gender non-conforming people of all ages.

  7. Trans Women of Color Collective ↗ twocc.org

    Black trans-led group in Kansas City that holds resource fairs and runs The Drop, a community hub and safe space in Westport.

  8. Education Equity Collective ↗ surjkc.org

    Kansas City group working on racial justice and educational equity; formerly SURJ KC, now Education Equity Collective, fiscally sponsored by Missouri Jobs with Justice Voter Action.

  9. Kansas City Defender ↗ kansascitydefender.com

    Black abolition-focused news outlet that also runs mutual aid programs

  10. Liberation Lit ↗ instagram.com

    Sending books to individuals incarcerated in Missouri and Kansas. We send books through bars to tear them down.

  11. Kansas City Incarcerated Workers Organizing Committee ↗ facebook.com

    This is the Greater Kansas City Incarcerated Workers Organizing Committee of the IWW.
