Norfolk

6 groups.
Part of Virginia.
  1. Our Angels Clothing Closetinstagram.com

    Norfolk clothing closet founded by Tiffany Adams that gives clothing, shoes and socks to people in the community at no cost.

  2. Keeping Us Secure & Prosperous (KUSP)instagram.com

    Norfolk nonprofit that expands emergency food access through community gardens, community fridges and pantries, mobile distributions and workshops.

  3. 757 Community Fridgesinstagram.com

    A mutual aid initiative providing free food access through community refrigerators in Norfolk and the Hampton Roads area. They work to address food insecurity through direct community support and solidarity, maintaining public fridges that anyone can access 24/7.

  4. Eleanor's Norfolkeleanorsnfk.com

    An intersectional feminist bookstore and bottle shop that serves as a community space and supports local mutual aid networks. They amplify traditionally silenced voices through literature and host community events while participating in the Hampton Roads Mutual Aid Network.

  5. Southeastern Trans Centersoutheasterntranscenter.org

    Norfolk-based center offering resources, events and support for transgender people across Hampton Roads, and running Lamia House, the region's first trans shelter.

  6. Humanities Behind Barshumanitiesbehindbars.org

    Abolitionist collective in Tidewater, Virginia, offering political education, a pen pal program with incarcerated people, and an Abolition School.
